# Troubleshooting Guide

## High spend and no conversions

Check conversion tracking, form submission, phone link, WhatsApp link, booking flow, thank-you page, UTM parameters, and mobile page speed. Reduce to one primary conversion action. Review search terms and exclude irrelevant phrases. Match the landing headline to the ad promise.

## Many leads but poor quality

Add qualification questions. State service area and starting price where appropriate. Add negative keywords such as jobs, free, course, DIY, salary, supplier, and unrelated city names. Track qualified CPL rather than raw CPL.

## Low click-through rate

Improve the offer, language, and creative. Mention city or service area. Replace slogans with concrete benefit, proof, price condition, or availability. Test one pain-led message and one proof-led message.

## Good CTR and low conversion rate

Fix landing-page mismatch, weak trust signals, long forms, missing VAT/delivery/cancellation details, slow mobile speed, and broken RTL layout. Move CTA above the fold. Add license, reviews, address, opening hours, and service area when relevant.

## ROI looks profitable but cash flow is weak

Recalculate using gross profit. Deduct refunds, cancellations, delivery issues, discounts, sales labor, and late payment. Separate new and returning customers. Compare platform conversions with CRM/orders.

## Hebrew copy feels unnatural

Use simple active Hebrew. Prefer plural-neutral CTA. Remove English structure, exaggerated slogans, and unnatural gender agreement. Add Israeli proof: city, availability, license, reviews, price conditions, and service area.

## Arabic or Russian leads cannot be served

Pause the language variant, add routing, or provide service capability. Do not keep low-CPL leads that cannot convert due to language handling.

## Platform disapproval

Remove regulated claims, personal-attribute wording, unsupported guarantees, before/after creative, restricted product wording, trademark issues, or landing-page conflicts. Keep a neutral backup creative.

## Direct-message compliance risk

Verify consent, sender identity, commercial message marking, and removal mechanism. Separate service follow-up from promotional messaging. Keep consent evidence.

## Remarketing risk

Pause until privacy notice, audience source, legal basis/consent, and retention are reviewed. Avoid sensitive audiences and minors. Use search/contextual alternatives if risk remains high.

## Budget fragmentation

Merge small ad sets, reduce languages, reduce platforms, and pick one primary channel. Add channels only after conversion economics are stable.

## Price complaints

Show total consumer price where required. Clarify VAT, delivery, valid dates, minimum purchase, inventory limits, cancellation, and warranty near the CTA.

## Accessibility issue

Add labels, keyboard navigation, contrast, error messages, captions, alt text, and correct right-to-left layout. Test on mobile and desktop.
