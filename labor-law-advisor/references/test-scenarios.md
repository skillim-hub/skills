# Test Scenarios

Use these scenarios to test responses, calculations, edge-case handling, and safe escalation. Each scenario includes expected guidance themes, not a guaranteed legal outcome.

## 1. Part-time minimum wage shortfall
Facts: 50% position, salary ₪3,000, pay month 04-2026. Web-validated full-time minimum ₪6,443.85 from 01/04/2026.
Expected: required ₪3,221.93, shortfall ₪221.93, verify official rate, check deductions and overtime separately.

## 2. Hourly minimum wage shortfall
Facts: hourly wage ₪34.00, 120 regular hours in 04-2026.
Expected: compare to ₪35.40, shortfall ₪168.00, do not include overtime as base wage.

## 3. Hourly wage compliant
Facts: hourly wage ₪38.00, 100 regular hours.
Expected: compliant with default hourly minimum; still check overtime, pension, travel, vacation, and sick leave.

## 4. Daily overtime
Facts: ₪40/hour, 11 hours worked, daily threshold 8.6.
Expected: 8.6 regular, 2 hours at 125%, 0.4 at 150%, total ₪468.00.

## 5. No daily overtime but weekly overtime
Facts: five days of 8.4 hours and one day of 4 hours, total 46 hours.
Expected: check weekly threshold of 42 and apply without double-counting.

## 6. Saturday work
Facts: employee's weekly rest is Saturday, worked 6 hours.
Expected: flag weekly rest premium and permit issue; ask about religion/weekly rest and sector.

## 7. Global overtime clause
Facts: contract says salary includes all overtime; no separate payslip line.
Expected: flag anti-pattern; check whether global overtime component is separate, real, reasonable, and documented.

## 8. Severance after 3 years and 4 months
Facts: dismissal, salary ₪12,000, tenure 3 years 4 months.
Expected: estimate ₪40,000; check Section 14 and fund balance; request Form 161 and release letters.

## 9. Section 14 partial top-up
Facts: statutory estimate ₪40,000, fund severance balance ₪35,000, valid coverage uncertain.
Expected: possible top-up ₪5,000 if Section 14 does not fully replace; check coverage period and rate.

## 10. Resignation for childcare
Facts: parent resigns after birth to care for child.
Expected: explain possible resignation treated as dismissal; check timing, reason, notice, and documents.

## 11. Resignation after pay cut
Facts: employer cut salary by 15%, employee resigned two months later.
Expected: check worsening conditions, objection, chance to fix, and evidence.

## 12. Pregnant employee dismissal
Facts: employee pregnant, employer wants dismissal for performance.
Expected: check tenure and permit requirement; stop action until permit analysis; escalate.

## 13. Fertility treatment absences
Facts: employee absent for fertility treatments and receives warning.
Expected: check statutory protection and documentation; preserve messages.

## 14. Return from birth and parenting period
Facts: employee returns and hours are reduced.
Expected: flag protected return period and possible permit/discrimination issue.

## 15. Sick leave pay
Facts: daily wage ₪500, 5 sick days, enough accrued balance.
Expected: day 1 ₪0, days 2-3 ₪250 each, days 4-5 ₪500 each, total ₪1,500.

## 16. Sick leave with insufficient balance
Facts: employee has 2 accrued sick days but absent 5 days.
Expected: pay only available statutory/accrued entitlement unless contract is more generous.

## 17. Vacation for 6th year
Facts: 6 years seniority, 5-day week.
Expected: statutory net working days: 14; check part-time proration and collective agreement.

## 18. Convalescence pay first year
Facts: employee completes 12 months in private sector.
Expected: entitlement begins after one year; verify current daily rate and temporary deductions.

## 19. No pension for 8 months
Facts: employee had active pension before hire, employer started deposits only after month 8.
Expected: check prior pension rule and retroactivity; request fund reports.

## 20. Invoice worker at one company
Facts: freelancer invoices one company, works fixed hours at company office with manager.
Expected: high misclassification risk; estimate exposure for minimum wage, overtime, pension, vacation, severance.

## 21. Consumer hires cleaner weekly
Facts: private household pays cleaner cash weekly for two years.
Expected: possible direct employer obligations; track minimum wage, pension, vacation, sick leave, convalescence, severance.

## 22. Histadrut dues on payslip
Facts: payslip shows union dues and worker asks about study fund.
Expected: check collective agreement/extension order; do not assume every right from dues alone.

## 23. Cleaning contractor at client site
Facts: cleaner works for contractor in office building.
Expected: check cleaning sector extension order and service-contractor rules.

## 24. Youth employee
Facts: 17-year-old works summer shifts.
Expected: verify youth minimum wage table and youth work restrictions.

## 25. Missing payslips
Facts: employee paid by bank transfer, no payslips for six months.
Expected: flag Wage Protection Law record issue and reconstruct from evidence.

## 26. Deductions for register shortage
Facts: cashier pays ₪300 shortage deduction.
Expected: check written consent and legal basis; ensure minimum wage not breached.

## 27. Fixed-term non-renewal during pregnancy
Facts: six-month contract not renewed after pregnancy disclosure.
Expected: treat as potential dismissal/protected action and check permit requirement.

## 28. Employer asks for waiver
Facts: employee asked to sign waiver of vacation and overtime.
Expected: statutory rights generally cannot be waived below minimum.

## 29. Public-sector convalescence
Facts: municipal employee asks about daily convalescence rate.
Expected: public sector may have a different rate; verify collective/circular source.

## 30. Foreign remote employer
Facts: Israeli resident works from Tel Aviv for a foreign company.
Expected: Israeli labor law may apply depending on facts; escalate for cross-border review.
