# Workflow Guide

## Workflow 1: Apartment feasibility

Input: city, area, scope, finish, bathrooms, kitchens, building year, floor, elevator, occupancy, VAT preference.

Steps: classify as concept estimate; choose per-m² benchmark; add room checks; apply city/access/age/occupied factors; add contingency; apply VAT; list missing data; generate contractor RFQ checklist.

Output example: "Planning budget: ₪430,000–₪620,000 including VAT. Primary risks: old plumbing, no-elevator logistics, bathroom waterproofing, waste removal."

## Workflow 2: Contractor quote normalization

1. Normalize VAT.
2. Add excluded items.
3. Convert lines to m², point, room, or lump sum.
4. Mark allowances.
5. Check payment schedule.
6. Check warranty, insurance, and licensed trades.
7. Rank by completeness before price.

## Workflow 3: Clinic fit-out

Use commercial benchmark. Add business licensing, fire safety, accessibility, privacy/acoustics, signage, low-voltage, landlord approval, and VAT cash-flow view.

## Workflow 4: Retail shop

Use retail benchmark. Add shopfront, display lighting, signage approval, accessibility, fire safety, opening-date risk, and lease reinstatement obligations.

## Workflow 5: Landlord make-ready

Use cosmetic benchmark plus known paint/floor/bathroom quantities. Separate mandatory repairs from optional upgrades. Keep a contingency line for hidden bathroom issues.

## Workflow 6: Water damage

Separate insurance restoration from elective upgrades. Add drying, moisture testing, demolition, mold treatment, temporary accommodation, and high uncertainty.

## Workflow 7: Shell-to-finish

Use shell benchmark. Add partitions, electrical, plumbing, HVAC, flooring, doors, kitchen, wet rooms, design, supervision, and building-management coordination.

## Workflow 8: Change order

Require written reason, original contract comparison, quantity, unit price, VAT, schedule impact, photos, and approval. Do not accept oral changes.

## Workflow 9: Payment milestones

Typical pattern: 5%–10% mobilization, 10%–15% after demolition, 20%–25% rough systems, 20%–25% finishes, 20%–25% carpentry/fixtures, 5%–10% retained for defects.

## Workflow 10: Final report

Include project facts, estimate class, source assumptions, scope, table, VAT, contingency, compliance flags, quote checklist, and decision recommendation.


## Official-source note

Use official sources for VAT, price-index movement, permits, business licensing, accessibility, fire safety, health, waste, asbestos, and standards. Use per-meter and per-room amounts only as planning heuristics until written contractor quotes are received.
