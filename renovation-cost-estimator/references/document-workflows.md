# Document Workflows

## Feasibility brief

Sections: project, scope, assumptions, budget range, VAT, main risks, next actions.

## Contractor request for quote

Include property details, access, occupancy, demolition, flooring, wet rooms, kitchen, electrical, plumbing, HVAC, doors, paint, waste removal, protection, final cleaning, quantities, VAT status, material models, schedule, milestones, warranty, insurance, and licensed trades.

## Quote comparison memo

Compare total before VAT, VAT included, total including VAT, missing items, allowances, payment front-loading, warranty, insurance, licensed trades, and risk rating.

## Change-order form

Fields: number, date, reason, original contract coverage, detailed scope, quantity, unit price, total before VAT, VAT, total including VAT, schedule impact, evidence, approval.

## Business fit-out compliance memo

Sections: business use, municipality, lease approval, business license, accessibility, fire safety, signage, health rules, opening-date risk, budget allowances, consultant review.

## Tax/accounting evidence pack

Include contract, valid tax invoices, payment confirmations, before/after photos, repair/improvement split, landlord approvals, and notes for accountant.

## Building or landlord approval pack

Include work description, contractor insurance, work hours, elevator protection, waste plan, noise/dust controls, contact person, schedule, permits, and restoration commitments.

## Closeout file

Include final invoices, warranties, as-built notes, manuals, paint codes, waterproofing details, inspection records, defect list, and final payment approval.

## Localization

Use ₪285,000, 31/12/2026, 72 מ״ר, חשבונית מס, מע״מ, רישיון עסק, היתר בנייה, נגישות, כיבוי אש.


## Official-source note

Use official sources for VAT, price-index movement, permits, business licensing, accessibility, fire safety, health, waste, asbestos, and standards. Use per-meter and per-room amounts only as planning heuristics until written contractor quotes are received.
