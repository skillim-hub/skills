# Troubleshooting

## Total too low

Likely causes: VAT omitted, kitchen/bathroom omitted, demolition/waste missing, access not priced, electrical/plumbing upgrades missing, contingency too low, commercial scope priced as residential.

Fix: normalize VAT, add wet-room and kitchen allowances, add access multiplier, add infrastructure, increase contingency, and reclassify scope.

## Total too high

Likely causes: per-m² and room benchmarks counted together, every multiplier applied to every line, premium finish selected accidentally, overhead duplicated, VAT applied twice.

Fix: isolate base scope, treat room ranges as checks or partial add-ons, apply multipliers selectively, and recalculate VAT once.

## Exact price requested

Respond with a range and list required data: measurements, photos, site access, material level, contractor proposals, VAT status, and schedule.

## Unknown finish

Map cheap/simple/rental to basic; normal/decent/family apartment to standard; designer/high quality to premium; luxury/custom/imported to luxury.

## Unknown scope

Map paint/refresh to cosmetic; bathroom/kitchen/floors to partial; everything/full gut to full; empty shell to shell; office to office_fitout; shop to retail_fitout; clinic/public business to commercial_fitout.

## Quote lacks VAT status

Show both outcomes and require written clarification.

## Quote lacks quantities

Request m² flooring, wall tile, electrical points, plumbing points, doors/windows, bathroom and kitchen specifications, demolition, waste, and schedule assumptions.

## Old building

Add 15%–30% contingency and require inspection for pipes, electrical, dampness, asbestos suspicion, structural changes, and access.

## Occupied work

Add protection, cleaning, temporary services, furniture movement, reduced productivity, and phased schedule.

## Commercial licensing

Add municipal check, fire safety, accessibility, signage, landlord approval, Ministry of Health where relevant, ventilation, grease trap, and schedule buffer.

## VAT for business

Show pre-VAT, VAT amount, and cash outlay. State that recoverability depends on valid tax invoices and accounting review.

## Structural changes

Add engineer review, permit flag, temporary support risk, schedule allowance, and do not price as simple demolition.

## Food business

Add ventilation, grease trap, washable surfaces, floor drains, pest-control considerations, health/municipal review, and fire safety.

## Clinic

Add accessibility, privacy/acoustics, washable surfaces where needed, handwash points, communications, signage, licensing, and health rules by practice type.

## Inflation update

Use official construction input index when available: updated_cost = original_cost * current_index / original_index.

## Output too vague

Add assumption table, included/excluded scope, low/expected/high table, next data needed, and decision recommendation.


## Official-source note

Use official sources for VAT, price-index movement, permits, business licensing, accessibility, fire safety, health, waste, asbestos, and standards. Use per-meter and per-room amounts only as planning heuristics until written contractor quotes are received.
