## Method

Performed two independent web-validation passes for every official or regulatory claim used by the package. Pass 1 used the most direct official source. Pass 2 used a different official page or, where no second regulator page existed, a different official page from the same authority that validated the same claim from another angle.

Status values:

- `` means double-confirmed.
- `→` means corrected after pass 1 or pass 2 and now aligned.
- `final ` means not confirmed by either pass.
