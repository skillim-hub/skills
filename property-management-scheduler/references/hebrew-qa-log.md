# Hebrew QA Log

Review date: 2026-06-04

## Review rules

1. Use natural Israeli professional Hebrew.
2. Use neutral imperative voice.
3. Avoid nikud in technical prose.
4. Prefer real Hebrew terminology over English transliteration.
5. Use ₪ and DD/MM/YYYY date formatting in Hebrew examples.
6. Avoid pretending to provide legal, tax, insurance, or engineering advice.
