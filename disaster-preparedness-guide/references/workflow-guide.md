# Workflow Guide

## 1. Household readiness in one evening

Walk the home; choose primary and backup protected spaces; time routes in daylight and darkness; clear obstacles; place water, flashlight, charger, radio, medication, paper contacts, IDs, pet supplies, and baby supplies; assign roles; write the plan on one page; practice calmly; review monthly.

Acceptance criteria: everyone knows where to go, route is clear, medication and glasses are reachable, and the plan works without one specific phone.

## 2. Apartment building or vaad bayit

Identify shared shelters; check doors, lighting, ventilation, keys, signage, and accessibility; create a resident assistance list with consent and minimal data; assign floor contacts; report locked shelters to the municipality; run a short drill; publish emergency numbers without private medical details.

## 3. Retail shop during missile alert

Before: mark route, train staff announcement, keep aisles clear, define POS stop rule, place first-aid kit.

During: shift lead announces sheltering, staff escort customers, checkout stops, merchandise stays, door closes only if safe, everyone sits low away from glass.

After: wait for appropriate release, check injuries and damage, restart only if safe, record closure time, stock loss, staff hours, canceled orders, and ₪ amount.

## 4. Freelancer outside home

Before: know shelters at frequent locations, backup work, prepare delay template, carry charger/ID/medication.

During: follow site instructions; if outside use nearest building or lie away from glass/vehicles; do not protect equipment before life safety.

After: update client when safe, record lost time, save screenshots, adjust invoice/project dates, ask accountant if loss is material.

## 5. Earthquake in clinic or office

Before: secure shelves/equipment, keep exits clear, identify open assembly area, train that earthquake does not mean sealed mamad, assign patient assistance roles.

During: open ground if seconds away; otherwise stairwell or mamad with door open; otherwise Drop-Cover-Hold.

After: no elevators, check injuries, shut hazards if safe, evacuate damaged building, record patients/staff present, resume appointments only after safety check.

## 6. Hazmat near industrial zone

Before: keep chemical inventory and safety data sheets where required; identify upper-floor shelter-in-place room; train staff not to mix or wash unknown substances.

During: move indoors/upward, close ventilation, keep customers away from exposed goods, call 102 for direct danger.

After: do not clean unknown residue, document stock damage when safe, contact insurer/landlord/accountant if needed.

## 7. Pet-inclusive household plan

Keep leash/carrier by the protected space; practice without chasing; add water bowl and waste bags; do not exceed the available shelter time searching for a hiding animal; after the event check paws and stress symptoms.

## 8. Reopening after disruption

Confirm official restrictions lifted; inspect structure, electricity, water, sanitation, ventilation, access, stock, payment systems, and staffing; notify customers; save an incident file with DD/MM/YYYY date, timeline, photos, invoices, receipts, staff hours, canceled orders, insurer/accountant correspondence, and ₪ estimate; review within 72 hours.
