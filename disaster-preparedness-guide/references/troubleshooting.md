# Troubleshooting and Recovery Guide

## Active alert problems

| Problem | Immediate action | Later fix |
|---|---|---|
| Siren but phone silent | Treat as real and enter protected space | Check app permissions, selected areas, volume, battery optimization |
| Phone alert but no siren | Follow official alert if area applies | Confirm location/areas |
| Customer refuses to move | Give one clear instruction and point to route | Add signage and multilingual script |
| Door blocked | Use next safest area | Remove storage; add weekly check |
| Not enough room | Move everyone away from glass and use overflow | Recalculate capacity |
| Person freezes | Use short commands: stand, come, sit | Assign buddy support |
| Pet hides | Do not exceed shelter time | Practice carrier routine |

## After missile or drone event

Broken glass: keep shoes on, move children/pets away, photograph only when safe. Suspicious fragments: do not touch; call 100. Fire/smoke/gas: call 102. Injury: call 101. Business closure: post notice, preserve evidence, contact insurer/accountant when relevant.

## After earthquake

Gas smell: leave, avoid switches/flames, call 102. Cracks or shifted structure: evacuate and wait for professional assessment. Elevator stopped: do not force doors. Aftershock: Drop-Cover-Hold or stay in open area. Coastal location: move inland/high ground if tsunami signs or official alert.

## Hazmat/radiological

Unknown residue on clothes: remove outer layer carefully, bag, wash exposed skin gently. Outdoor plume: indoors/upward or upwind/uphill if outside. Food exposed outdoors: do not consume until cleared. Staff wants to clean spill: stop and wait for trained guidance.

## Digital and records recovery

Lost phone: use paper contacts and secondary alert channel. No internet: use radio/SMS/calls/offline plan. POS down: use approved manual log only if safe and legal; reconcile later. Missing invoices: restore from cloud, accountant, or email. Insurance deadline unclear: notify promptly and save communication.
