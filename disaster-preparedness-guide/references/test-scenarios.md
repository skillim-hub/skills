# Test Scenarios

1. Missile alert at night with children: enter mamad, close openings, sit low, wait for correct release.
2. Missile alert while showering: stop immediately and shelter; do not finish shower.
3. Top-floor apartment without mamad: use internal stairwell/safest room; avoid elevator.
4. Outdoor pedestrian near glass: enter building or lie away from glass/vehicles.
5. Driver on highway: stop safely, exit if safe, otherwise lower below window line.
6. Drone alert lasts longer: remain until official all-clear; do not film.
7. Earthquake in apartment with mamad: open ground if seconds away; otherwise mamad/stairwell with door open.
8. Earthquake near elevator: do not use elevator.
9. Earthquake near beach: after shaking, move inland/high if tsunami signs or alert.
10. Sea withdraws with no phone alert: treat as tsunami warning.
11. Hazmat plume in industrial area: indoors/upward, close ventilation, avoid basement unless instructed.
12. Radiological instruction: shelter indoors, close air, remove exposed outer clothing if needed.
13. Terrorist infiltration: lock, hide, silence, call 100 only when safe.
14. Older adult cannot reach shelter: closest safe interior area and neighbor check-in.
15. Wheelchair user blocked by narrow door: accessible alternative and buddy plan.
16. Hearing-impaired resident asleep: vibration/visual alerts and redundant buddy channel.
17. Autistic child panics: visual routine, calm commands, headphones if helpful.
18. Pet hides: do not exceed shelter time; improve carrier practice.
19. Coffee shop with customers: stop service and escort customers first.
20. Clinic with wheelchair patient: accessible route and dignity-preserving support.
21. Workshop with chemicals: shelter people first; do not clean unknown chemicals.
22. Freelancer misses deadline: notify when safe, document lost ₪ and evidence.
23. Food shop loses refrigeration: assess stock safety and document loss.
24. Storefront glass shattered: keep people away, photograph only when safe, reopen only when safe.
25. Employee posts live security-force location: remove, prohibit live security details.
26. POS and internet fail: use approved manual process only if safe/legal.
27. Landlord pushes reopening despite cracks: wait for safety confirmation.
28. Customer demands pickup during closure: decline for safety and reschedule.
29. Unknown powder on inventory: isolate, do not clean, call 102 if danger.
30. Accounting file missing: restore from cloud/accountant/email and log gaps.
31. Lookup missile returns protected-space and official-release guidance.
32. Lookup Hebrew רעידת אדמה returns earthquake guidance and open-door warning.
33. Lookup hazmat returns upstairs/ventilation and basement warning.
34. Business plan with chemicals adds hazmat controls.
35. Household plan with pets adds leash/carrier.
36. Accessibility flag adds route/buddy checks.
37. Unknown hazard returns structured error.
