# Troubleshooting Guide

## Diagnosis sequence

1. Identify company number and current status.
2. Identify proposed or completed action.
3. Locate controlling law, articles, agreement, registers, and resolutions.
4. Compare company extract with internal records.
5. Identify approval route.
6. Identify missing filings, signatures, dates, and attachments.
7. Identify tax, bank, employment, consumer, privacy, and licensing dependencies.
8. Prepare correction path and escalation list.

## Company name rejected

Symptoms: incorporation or name-change request returned. Causes: similar existing name, misleading wording, protected term, government/professional implication, mismatch between Hebrew and English name. Fix: prepare alternatives, remove restricted words, use distinctive element, and check trademark/domain separately.

## Signature defect

Symptoms: filing returned for missing or invalid signature. Causes: wrong signatory, missing director consent, outdated form, unsupported electronic signature, foreign signature issue. Fix: confirm signer, use current form, match IDs, re-sign correctly, and handle foreign notarization/apostille through counsel.

## Cap table mismatch

Symptoms: spreadsheet differs from register, investor asks for allotment approvals, founder claims oral equity, transfer deed missing. Fix: build chronological list of all allotments, transfers, cancellations, conversions, options, waivers, and approvals; rebuild register; obtain legal review before ratification.

## Non-compliant company status

Symptoms: extract shows warning, bank/tender/investor refuses to proceed, annual fee or report missing. Fix: retrieve extract, list missing years, file annual reports, verify fees, correct address/director/shareholder records, archive confirmations.

## Director resignation not reflected

Symptoms: former director appears in extract or still has authority. Causes: resignation not recorded, external update not filed, bank permissions not updated. Fix: locate notice, confirm date, update register, file update, remove access, notify insurer.

## Shareholder agreement conflicts with articles

Symptoms: drag-along exists only in agreement, transfer restrictions differ, future shareholders not bound. Fix: create conflict table, amend articles through proper approval route, sign deeds of adherence, align cap table and registers.

## Bank rejects onboarding

Symptoms: corporate account not opened or bank asks for documents. Causes: beneficial ownership unclear, signing-rights resolution missing, high-risk activity, tax files missing, foreign ownership documentation incomplete. Fix: prepare certificate, articles, extract, cap table, IDs, signing-rights resolution, activity statement, source-of-funds explanation, licenses, and tax confirmations.

## Dormant company still receives demands

Symptoms: no income but annual fee demands continue. Causes: corporate obligations continue until proper closure or exemption; tax and corporate status are separate. Fix: check Registrar status, CPA tax status, bank accounts, contracts, reports, fees, and liquidation option.

## Share transfer creates tax surprise

Symptoms: transfer for ₪1, family transfer, founder buyback, or option promise. Causes: no tax review, market value issues, employment equity, foreign residency, reverse-vesting error. Fix: pause closing, document consideration, check withholding, separate employment compensation from share sale, store tax memo.

## Consumer-facing company has personal exposure

Symptoms: owner signs personally, website lists wrong entity, refund terms violate consumer law. Fix: use company name and number, avoid personal guarantees where possible, update terms, invoices, privacy notice, refund policy, and insurance.

## Escalation checklist

Escalate when a deadline passed, ownership is disputed, a director conflict exists, debts cannot be paid, charge filing is late, foreign parties are involved, regulated activity began without license, securities were offered, privacy exposure is significant, or consumer class-action risk exists.


## Problem: dated fee or tax figure is used without verification

Symptoms: an invoice uses the wrong VAT rate, annual fee is paid after the reduced-rate deadline, or an exempt-dealer threshold from a prior year is reused.

Likely causes: old template, cached article, copied spreadsheet, or advice that did not state an access date.

Fix:

1. Check the official Tax Authority, Companies Authority, or relevant regulator page.
2. Record the access date.
3. Update the workflow and client record.
4. Treat the 02/06/2026 validated values as examples only after the official source is rechecked.
5. Escalate to a CPA or Israeli lawyer when tax classification, late filing, or sanctions are possible.
