# End-to-End Workflow Guide

## Workflow 1: Incorporate an Israeli private company

Inputs: business description, founder IDs, proposed names, registered office, share structure, directors, articles, IP history, regulated-activity screen.

Steps:

1. Confirm structure against individual business and partnership.
2. Screen for licensing, consumer, privacy, employment, and tax dependencies.
3. Prepare at least three Hebrew names.
4. Design simple share capital unless special rights are needed.
5. Prepare articles and initial resolutions.
6. Collect director consents and founder declarations.
7. Submit through the current Companies Authority process.
8. Open statutory books after registration.
9. Approve signing rights.
10. Prepare bank onboarding file.
11. Coordinate tax registrations.
12. Create annual compliance calendar.

Deliverables: incorporation checklist, cap table, initial resolutions, statutory registers, bank-signing resolution, post-incorporation task list.

Acceptance criteria: company number received, articles saved, registers opened, signing-rights resolution signed, tax responsibility assigned, annual reminders created.

## Workflow 2: Prepare shareholder-agreement issue list

Inputs: cap table, founder roles, investment plan, board preferences, exit expectations, IP ownership, loans, family or foreign facts.

Steps:

1. Reconcile cap table against records.
2. List founder contributions.
3. Select governance model.
4. Define reserved matters.
5. Define transfer restrictions.
6. Define leaver and vesting rules.
7. Add deadlock mechanism.
8. Add information rights.
9. Add related-party approval rules.
10. Align articles and require deed of adherence.
11. Mark tax, employment, securities, and non-compete questions for counsel.

Deliverables: issue list, risk table, lawyer questions, articles alignment list, signing dependencies.

Acceptance criteria: no cap table mismatch, deadlock process exists for equal founders, future shareholders must adhere, tax flags identified.

## Workflow 3: File annual report and clean status

Inputs: company number, extract, directors, shareholders, registered office, share changes, auditor details, fee status.

Steps:

1. Retrieve company extract.
2. Compare extract with internal registers.
3. Reconcile share events.
4. Reconcile director changes.
5. Confirm registered office.
6. Check annual fee and report history.
7. Prepare missing updates.
8. Submit annual report.
9. Archive confirmation and receipts.
10. Update next deadline.

Deliverables: status memo, reconciled cap table, filing confirmation, missing-documents log, calendar update.

## Workflow 4: Transfer shares

Inputs: articles, shareholder agreement, register, cap table, seller, buyer, share class, quantity, price, tax advice status.

Steps:

1. Confirm seller ownership.
2. Identify transfer restrictions.
3. Obtain waivers and approvals.
4. Prepare transfer deed.
5. Obtain deed of adherence if needed.
6. Complete tax review.
7. Update register and cap table after closing.
8. File externally where required.
9. Archive documents.

Deliverables: approval matrix, transfer deed list, updated register, updated cap table.

## Workflow 5: Change directors and signing rights

Inputs: articles, current directors register, consent or resignation, approval route, bank policy, insurance.

Steps:

1. Determine authority.
2. Collect signed document.
3. Approve through correct organ.
4. Update directors register.
5. File update where required.
6. Update bank authority only if intended.
7. Update insurance and access.
8. Archive records.

Deliverables: resolution, updated register, filing confirmation, bank action list, access checklist.

## Workflow 6: Investor due diligence

Inputs: certificate, articles, shareholder agreement, deeds of adherence, minutes, cap table, registers, option grants, IP assignments, material contracts, tax confirmations, charges.

Steps:

1. Build folder index.
2. Reconcile cap table.
3. Confirm issuances and transfers were approved.
4. Check annual reports and fees.
5. Check charges.
6. Check IP chain of title.
7. Check founder and contractor confidentiality.
8. Identify missing signatures.
9. Prepare remediation plan.

Deliverables: due-diligence folder, missing-signature list, cleanup plan, risk memo, counsel questions.

## Workflow 7: Dormant company cleanup

Inputs: company status, annual report history, annual fee status, bank account, tax files, debts, contracts, shareholders, directors.

Steps:

1. Confirm assets, debts, employees, contracts, bank accounts, and tax files.
2. Check Registrar status.
3. File missing annual reports if needed.
4. Verify annual fees. For 2026, the validation pass found ₪1,338 reduced company fee until 31/03/2026 and ₪1,777 from 01/04/2026; recheck before payment.
5. Coordinate tax status with CPA.
6. Decide whether to keep, sell, merge, or liquidate.
7. Use legal guidance for liquidation.
8. Archive confirmations.

Deliverables: dormancy memo, compliance checklist, tax coordination list, liquidation decision memo.
