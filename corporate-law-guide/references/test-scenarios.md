# Test Scenarios

Use these scenarios to test answers and the helper client. Expected handling is a workflow direction, not legal advice.

1. Solo graphic designer earns ₪90,000: compare individual business and company; flag VAT/tax status.
2. Cybersecurity consultant signs large liability contracts: investigate company, insurance, contract caps, personal guarantees.
3. Two equal founders demand unanimous approval: warn about deadlock and add mechanism.
4. Founder wrote code before incorporation: add IP assignment and contribution record.
5. US resident owns 30%: flag identity, tax residency, banking, sanctions, foreign-law review.
6. Shareholder transfers 10% to spouse for ₪1: check articles, agreement, permitted transfer, deed of adherence, tax.
7. Director resigns by WhatsApp: locate formal notice, update register, file update, remove access.
8. Dormant company for three years: explain continuing obligations; check reports, fees, tax, liquidation.
9. Name includes Bank: warn of protected term and likely rejection.
10. Drag-along only in shareholder agreement: align articles and deeds of adherence.
11. Seed investor diligence: create folder, reconcile cap table, IP, annual compliance.
12. Transfer approved only by email: flag defect; reconstruct timeline; counsel before ratification.
13. Bank refuses onboarding: prepare cap table, IDs, ownership declaration, resolutions, tax files.
14. Founder leaves after six months: check vesting, leaver clauses, IP, tax.
15. Online seller has no cancellation policy: update consumer terms, invoices, privacy.
16. Director spouse provides services: check related-party approval and market terms.
17. Registered office outdated: update records and filing; check notice risk.
18. Unpaid annual fee before tender: verify status, pay/file, archive confirmation.
19. Employee promised 5% options by email: check option plan, board approval, tax route.
20. Company cannot pay supplier debts: escalate insolvency; avoid continued trading analysis without counsel.
21. Bank requires floating charge: prepare debenture, board approval, deadline, legal review.
22. Minority shareholder denied information: check law, articles, agreement, dispute risk.
23. Broad non-compete: flag Israeli enforceability concerns; counsel review.
24. Health data collection: privacy and sector regulation beyond company law.
25. Foreign contractor assigns code: verify assignment, moral rights, tax withholding, signatures.
26. English-only records: maintain accurate Hebrew regulator data and bilingual support.
27. Three sibling shareholders: separate employment, dividends, related-party approvals, valuation.
28. No share certificates: check register and articles; register is central.
29. Personal guarantee requested by landlord: explain separate liability can be bypassed by guarantee.
30. Owner wants to close company tomorrow: formal process, debts/tax/bank checks, no instant deletion.
