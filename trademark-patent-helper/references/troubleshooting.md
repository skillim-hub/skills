# Troubleshooting guide

## Intake problems

### "Register an idea"

Classify the asset:

- Brand name or logo: trademark.
- Technical solution: patent.
- Product appearance: design.
- Text, image, music, code: copyright/evidence.
- Business plan alone: usually not a trademark or patent filing.

### Company name confused with trademark

Explain:

- Company registration creates an entity.
- A domain points to an internet address.
- A trademark identifies goods/services from a commercial source.
- One does not automatically grant the others.

### "Worldwide protection"

Explain territoriality:

- Israeli filing protects only according to Israeli law.
- International mechanisms exist, but still depend on countries, classes, fees, and deadlines.

## Trademark troubleshooting

### Descriptive mark

Symptoms: direct product/service wording, quality claims, speed claims, geography, ingredients, target audience.

Actions:

1. Recommend a distinctive mark.
2. Consider stylized filing only with limited expectations.
3. Prepare acquired-distinctiveness evidence if substantial use exists.
4. Avoid claiming ordinary descriptive terms.

### Similar mark found

Actions:

1. Compare appearance, sound, meaning, and overall commercial impression.
2. Compare goods/services and trade channels.
3. Check status of earlier mark.
4. Consider narrowing goods/services.
5. Consider coexistence or consent only with legal advice.
6. Rebrand if risk is severe.

### Goods/services too broad

Symptoms: "all services", "all software", many unrelated classes, copied competitor wording.

Actions:

1. Ask what is actually sold or planned in good faith.
2. Map each item to a Nice class.
3. Remove speculative classes.
4. Use specific terms.
5. Warn that overbroad filing increases cost and risk.

### File upload rejected

Actions:

1. Check accepted file type.
2. Check file size.
3. Use a simple filename.
4. Remove encryption from PDF.
5. Remove transparent background if rejected.
6. Restart session if the portal timed out.
7. Save screenshots of errors.

### Translation/transliteration issue

Actions:

1. Provide exact original script.
2. Add transliteration.
3. Add meaning or state that the term is invented.
4. Search spelling variants.

## Patent troubleshooting

### Public disclosure occurred

Actions:

1. Stop further disclosure.
2. Create timeline: date, channel, audience, confidentiality terms, exact material.
3. Preserve copies.
4. Seek professional review.
5. Check whether undisclosed improvements remain.

### Invention is only an idea

Actions:

1. Request components, steps, architecture, parameters, diagrams, and examples.
2. Ask what is new over existing products.
3. Ask for prototype/test data.
4. Produce an invention disclosure template rather than claims.

### Software looks like business method

Actions:

1. Identify technical problem.
2. Identify technical effect.
3. Collect architecture and data flow.
4. Compare against ordinary automation.
5. Recommend patentability review.

### Ownership unclear

Red flags:

- Employee invented during work.
- Contractor built for client.
- Founder invented before incorporation.
- University or hospital resources used.
- Grant or investor agreement exists.

Actions:

1. Collect agreements.
2. Identify inventors separately from owners.
3. Prepare assignment path.
4. Avoid filing under uncertain owner without advice.

### Prior art looks close

Actions:

1. Create feature comparison chart.
2. Identify missing elements.
3. Check combinations of references.
4. Reframe novelty around supported differences.
5. Share known prior art with advisors.

## Portal and payment problems

### Payment failed or page froze

Actions:

1. Do not repeat payment blindly.
2. Save screenshot, date, exact time, amount in ₪, card last four digits, and transaction reference.
3. Check whether an application number was created.
4. Contact official support through official channels.

### Hebrew text displays incorrectly

Actions:

1. Use UTF-8 text.
2. Paste plain text.
3. Avoid hidden formatting from word processors.
4. Check punctuation direction.
5. Reopen generated PDF before upload.

## Escalation triggers

Escalate to professional review when:

- A cease-and-desist letter exists.
- Opposition or cancellation is involved.
- A deadline is close.
- International filing is planned.
- Public disclosure occurred.
- Ownership is disputed.
- Medical, defense, biotech, fintech, cannabis, security, or other regulated field is involved.
- A similar trademark exists.
- Enforcement against another party is planned.
