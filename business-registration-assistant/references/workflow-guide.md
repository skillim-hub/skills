# Workflow Guide

## Workflow 1: First-time עוסק פטור tutor

Facts: private tutoring, consumers, expected turnover ₪72,000, profit ₪5,000/month, start date 01/09/2026, no regulated profession.

Steps:

1. Verify current עוסק פטור ceiling.
2. Confirm the occupation is not excluded.
3. Collect ID and bank confirmation.
4. Prepare Hebrew activity description.
5. Estimate weekly hours and monthly profit.
6. Open VAT file as עוסק פטור if eligible.
7. Open income tax file.
8. Update Bituach Leumi.
9. Configure receipt software.
10. Track annual turnover monthly.

## Workflow 2: B2B consultant as עוסק מורשה

Facts: software consulting, companies as clients, turnover ₪220,000, clients require tax invoices.

Steps:

1. Treat as likely עוסק מורשה.
2. Prepare ID, bank confirmation, contracts, turnover and profit estimates.
3. Open VAT file as עוסק מורשה.
4. Open income tax file.
5. Update Bituach Leumi.
6. Configure חשבונית מס/קבלה.
7. State pricing as `₪X + מע״מ` or `₪X כולל מע״מ`.
8. Keep supplier tax invoices.
9. Calendar VAT reports.

## Workflow 3: Employee with side business

1. Collect employee salary, business profit, and weekly hours.
2. Confirm VAT eligibility independently.
3. Register with VAT and income tax.
4. Update Bituach Leumi as self-employed in addition to employment.
5. Check tax coordination.
6. Keep business expenses separate from employment reimbursements.

## Workflow 4: Digital marketplace seller

1. Determine whether the activity is goods, services, import/export, or mixed.
2. Calculate gross sales before platform fees.
3. Collect platform statements, payment processor reports, refunds, shipping, materials, fees, and currency conversions.
4. Review foreign-customer VAT treatment when relevant.
5. Register VAT status based on eligibility.
6. Open income tax file and update Bituach Leumi.
7. Create monthly bookkeeping folders.

## Workflow 5: Regulated profession below ceiling

1. Flag occupation restriction.
2. Do not rely on low turnover alone.
3. Verify official eligibility for עוסק פטור.
4. Prepare עוסק מורשה fallback.
5. Attach professional license.
6. Configure VAT invoice process if authorized dealer status applies.

## Workflow 6: Migration to עוסק מורשה

1. Calculate year-to-date turnover before expenses.
2. Forecast signed and likely contracts.
3. Compare with current ceiling.
4. Contact accountant or VAT office before crossing.
5. Confirm effective date.
6. Update invoice software.
7. Notify clients.
8. Clarify price plus VAT or including VAT.
9. Track input VAT only from the appropriate date and with valid invoices.
10. Update income tax and Bituach Leumi estimates.

## Workflow 7: Late registration

1. Record real start date and first payment date.
2. Gather deposits, contracts, messages, and unissued documents.
3. Do not invent a later start date.
4. Ask a professional how to reconstruct records.
5. Register or correct according to guidance.

## Workflow 8: Consumer checking a provider document

1. Ask whether provider is עוסק פטור or עוסק מורשה.
2. Explain that עוסק פטור provides a receipt and does not issue VAT invoice.
3. Explain that עוסק מורשה generally issues a VAT tax invoice or invoice-receipt.
4. Check serial number, date, business details, amount, and service description.

## Workflow 9: Accountant handoff

Create one packet:

```yaml
goal: "Open self-employed files"
requested_status: "osek_patur / osek_murshe / needs review"
activity_hebrew: ""
planned_start_date: "DD/MM/YYYY"
expected_annual_turnover_nis: 0
expected_monthly_profit_nis: 0
weekly_hours: 0
client_types: []
documents:
  - ID card and appendix
  - bank ownership confirmation
questions:
  - confirm current ceiling
  - confirm occupation eligibility
  - check income tax advances
  - check Bituach Leumi classification
```

## Workflow 10: Post-registration operating setup

1. Save authority confirmations.
2. Configure receipt/invoice software according to status.
3. Create income and expense folders.
4. Calendar VAT, annual declaration, income tax, and Bituach Leumi deadlines.
5. Reconcile bank deposits monthly.
6. Review turnover against ceiling monthly.
7. Review profit estimate quarterly.
8. Request certificates required by clients.


### Combined online opening routes

A live government announcement and a secondary procedural source confirm that some online routes can open a self-employed file across the Tax Authority and Bituach Leumi in one action. Still verify each resulting file separately: VAT status, income tax file, National Insurance classification, and contribution estimate.
