# Troubleshooting Guide

## Status problems

### User wants עוסק פטור but clients require חשבונית מס

Explain that עוסק פטור cannot issue a VAT tax invoice. Ask whether clients can accept a receipt. If not, consider עוסק מורשה and clarify pricing.

### User thinks low turnover guarantees עוסק פטור

Explain that the occupation must also be eligible. Regulated or listed professions may require עוסק מורשה even below the ceiling.

### User thinks עוסק פטור means no tax

State that the VAT status does not remove income tax or Bituach Leumi obligations.

### User crossed the ceiling

Calculate gross turnover before expenses, identify the likely crossing date, contact accountant/VAT office, update software, and stop relying on exempt-dealer assumptions for future transactions.

## Document problems

### Missing bank confirmation

Request bank ownership confirmation, bank letter, or cancelled check. For a joint account, show the registrant as an account holder.

### Address mismatch

Compare ID appendix, residential address, business address, and lease. Prepare a truthful explanation. Do not submit a false address.

### Missing professional license

Ask whether the activity requires a license. Treat עוסק פטור eligibility as uncertain until verified.

### Prior file existed

Collect prior VAT, income tax, and Bituach Leumi numbers, closure confirmation, open reports, debts, and credits.

## Timing problems

### Activity already started

Use actual dates. Gather payment evidence and contracts. Obtain professional review. Do not recommend backdating or a false future start date.

### User wants to wait until first payment

Explain that obligations may begin with business activity, contracts, advertising, or service delivery, not only payment. Prepare early.

## Bituach Leumi problems

### User is also salaried

Collect salary, business profit, and weekly hours. Report self-employed activity separately. Check tax coordination.

### User receives unemployment or benefits

Business activity can affect eligibility and reporting. Confirm with Bituach Leumi before activity or payment.

### Profit estimate is uncertain

Estimate revenue minus expected costs, mark as provisional, and update quarterly.

## Foreign and platform income

### Foreign clients

Collect contracts, customer location/status, place of benefit, service type, payment records, and currency conversion evidence. Require VAT review before applying zero-rate treatment.

### Platforms

Use gross sales, not only net payouts. Track platform fees, refunds, chargebacks, and exchange rates.

## Bookkeeping problems

### Wrong document issued

Check the status on the transaction date. Correct through bookkeeping software with an audit trail and professional guidance.

### Client asks for certificates

Explain אישור ניכוי מס במקור and אישור ניהול ספרים. Check the Tax Authority personal area or ask the representative.

## Escalation triggers

Escalate for regulated professions, foreign clients, import/export, late registration, prior debts, benefits, fast growth, business licensing, cash-heavy activity, or spouse/partner structures.
