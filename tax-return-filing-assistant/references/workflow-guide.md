# End-to-End Workflow Guide

## Workflow 1: Form 135 refund claim for a salaried employee

### Inputs

- Tax year.
- Form 106 from every employer.
- Bank account confirmation.
- Donation receipts with recognition under Section 46, if relevant.
- Pension, life insurance, disability, unemployment, maternity, reserve-duty, degree, settlement, or child-credit documents where relevant.
- Prior tax coordination approvals, if any.

### Steps

1. Confirm the taxpayer has salary income only and no full Form 1301 obligation.
2. Check the refund limitation period for the tax year.
3. Sum salary and tax withheld across all Forms 106.
4. Identify why withholding may have been excessive.
5. Map evidence to Form 135 fields.
6. Prepare attachment checklist.
7. Run validation:
   - missing employer Form 106,
   - duplicated credits,
   - donation receipt not recognized,
   - bank details missing,
   - old year near limitation deadline.
8. Prepare a filing package for official submission.

### Output checklist

- Completed Form 135 data summary.
- Form 106 list.
- Credit and deduction evidence.
- Refund bank confirmation.
- Planning deadline and official-verification note.

## Workflow 2: Form 1301 for a freelancer

### Inputs

- Tax year.
- Business revenue export.
- Expense ledger and invoices/receipts.
- Form 106, if the freelancer also had salary income.
- Form 867 or broker reports, if securities income exists.
- Rental records, if rental income exists.
- Foreign income statements, if relevant.
- Income tax prepayment and withholding confirmations.
- Pension, donation, insurance, and credit documents.

### Steps

1. Confirm the user is an individual or sole proprietor, not a company return.
2. Build the income map:
   - salary,
   - business/professional income,
   - rental,
   - capital gains,
   - foreign income,
   - other taxable income.
3. Build the deduction and credit map.
4. Reconcile business revenue to receipts, bank deposits, and VAT/exempt-occupation records where applicable.
5. Reconcile tax withheld and advances to Tax Authority account records.
6. Decide whether Form 6111 may be required.
7. Prepare Form 1301 field summary.
8. Prepare appendices and attachments.
9. Run validation before submission.

### Example

A consultant earned ₪240,000 from clients, paid ₪38,000 in expenses, received ₪60,000 salary from a part-time employer, and paid ₪18,000 in advances. Recommend Form 1301. Add salary from Form 106, business profit from the profit and loss report, advances, and pension/donation support.

## Workflow 3: Form 126 for a small employer

### Inputs

- Payroll system annual export.
- Monthly payroll registers.
- Forms 106 issued to employees.
- Withholding payment confirmations.
- National Insurance reports.
- Employee identity list and employment dates.
- Corrections made after year-end.

### Steps

1. Confirm the employer paid wages during the tax year.
2. Export annual payroll file from payroll software.
3. Compare annual totals to monthly payroll reports.
4. Compare Form 126 employee totals to Forms 106.
5. Reconcile withheld income tax to payment confirmations.
6. Reconcile National Insurance and health tax totals.
7. Identify terminated employees, negative corrections, and retroactive payments.
8. Prepare transmission package or adviser checklist.
9. Keep a correction workflow for post-submission changes.

### Red flags

- Employee ID fails validation.
- Employee appears twice with inconsistent totals.
- Form 106 was issued before a late correction.
- Gross salary differs from payroll ledger.
- Benefits in kind are missing.

## Workflow 4: Form 856 for supplier payments

### Inputs

- Supplier ledger cards.
- Payment register.
- Invoices and credit notes.
- Withholding certificates valid for payment dates.
- Monthly withholding payment confirmations.
- Supplier identity details.
- Payment type mapping.

### Steps

1. Identify non-employee recipients that may be reportable.
2. Exclude employee wages; those belong in Form 126.
3. Verify supplier legal identity.
4. Match each payment to a valid withholding certificate.
5. Split annual supplier records if withholding rate changed.
6. Reconcile gross payments to supplier ledgers.
7. Reconcile tax withheld to monthly payments.
8. Prepare Form 856 records.
9. Run validation for missing certificates, negative totals, foreign suppliers, and VAT base confusion.

### Example

A business paid Supplier A ₪50,000. The supplier had a 5% certificate until 30-06 and 0% from 01-07. Split the payments by payment date and apply the rate that was valid on each payment date.

## Workflow 5: Form 6111 from accounting software

### Inputs

- Closed trial balance.
- Profit and loss report.
- Balance sheet.
- Fixed asset and depreciation schedule.
- Inventory count and valuation.
- Accounts receivable/payable aging.
- Loan statements.
- Owner equity/drawing records.
- Tax adjustment schedule.
- Mapping from chart of accounts to Form 6111 item codes.

### Steps

1. Confirm the entity and tax year.
2. Confirm whether Form 6111 is required for the filing year.
3. Close bookkeeping entries for the year.
4. Map chart-of-account balances to Form 6111 codes.
5. Verify that profit and loss totals match the annual return.
6. Verify that balance sheet balances are internally consistent.
7. Reconcile turnover to VAT or exempt-occupation reports where relevant.
8. Reconcile salaries to Form 126, if employees exist.
9. Validate depreciation and non-deductible expenses.
10. Export from accounting software or prepare an adviser review package.

### Common reconciliation table

| Check | Expected result |
|---|---|
| Revenue in P&L vs annual return | Same or explainable by classification |
| Revenue vs VAT reports | Same, or explained by exempt/out-of-scope/timing items |
| Salaries vs Form 126 | Same taxable payroll basis or reconciled differences |
| Fixed assets vs depreciation schedule | Additions, disposals, and depreciation agree |
| Supplier balances vs Form 856 | Reportable payments reconciled or explained |

## Combined workflow: freelancer with employees and suppliers

Use this sequence:

1. Prepare bookkeeping close.
2. Prepare payroll close and Form 126.
3. Prepare supplier withholding close and Form 856.
4. Prepare Form 6111 if required.
5. Prepare Form 1301.
6. Reconcile totals across all forms.
7. Prepare final submission checklist.

Do not submit Form 1301 before major Form 126/856/6111 totals are stable, because downstream corrections can change the annual return.
