# Workflow Guide

## Workflow 1: New employee onboarding

Inputs: role, sector, start date, salary basis, expected hours, prior pension status, travel route, contract draft, onboarding forms.

Steps:

1. Classify worker and sector.
2. Check sector expansion order.
3. Confirm minimum wage and overtime structure.
4. Confirm weekly rest and night-work pattern.
5. Confirm pension start timing and contribution percentages.
6. Add vacation, sick leave, holiday, convalescence, and travel wording.
7. Remove waiver language and invalid penalty clauses.
8. Create payslip fields and attendance process.
9. Issue employment terms notice where required.
10. Store signed documents.

Acceptance criteria: no wage below verified minimum; no blanket waiver; pension timing documented; overtime policy matches actual role; sector-specific requirements checked.

## Workflow 2: Monthly payroll audit

Inputs: payroll month, payslips, attendance report, overtime approvals, leave balances, pension contribution report, travel reimbursement report.

Steps: reconcile attendance with payslips; identify unpaid training, closing, inter-site travel, on-call, and rest-day work; recalculate regular hours and overtime; compare hourly equivalents to minimum wage; check deductions; check pension deposits; check leave balances; check convalescence and travel; produce an exception report; correct payroll before final payment where possible.

Red flags: negative balances used as penalties; missing pension deposit after eligibility; manual deduction without legal basis; unexplained wage-adjustment lines; no holiday-pay logic for eligible hourly employees; repeated work above caps.

## Workflow 3: Overtime policy rollout

Inputs: role list, expected workload, manager approval flow, attendance method, draft policy, payroll wage components.

Steps:

1. Identify exempt and non-exempt roles cautiously.
2. Reject title-only exemptions.
3. Set approval process.
4. Define timekeeping requirements.
5. Define rest-day work process and permit check.
6. Define global overtime only with separate legal/payroll review.
7. Train managers not to request off-clock work.
8. Add reporting route for missed time.
9. Audit first two payroll cycles.

Problem wording: "Salary includes all overtime and no additional payment is owed."

Replacement direction: state regular salary separately from any approved overtime component, require accurate time records, and pay statutory overtime premiums where required.

## Workflow 4: Termination

Inputs: start date, tenure, reason, protected-status facts, performance records, contract, payslips, pension and severance records.

Steps: freeze final decision; check protected status; check permit needs; draft hearing invitation; allow response time and representation; hold good-faith hearing; consider response; issue written decision; calculate notice; calculate final payroll, vacation redemption, pension release, severance, and forms; provide employment-period confirmation; preserve records.

Do not issue a final termination letter before hearing, terminate during protected periods without permit analysis, label a dismissal as resignation, withhold final wages as leverage, or demand waiver of mandatory rights as a condition for statutory payments.

## Workflow 5: Pregnancy, fertility treatment, parental leave, and return to work

Inputs: event type and dates, tenure, current role, proposed change, business reason, prior communications, minimal documentation.

Steps: treat the matter as sensitive; separate business need from protected facts; avoid adverse change until permit and legal analysis are complete; review protected periods; consider accommodations without stereotypes; document objective reasons; communicate narrowly; escalate before termination, demotion, shift reduction, pay reduction, or refusal to return.

## Workflow 6: Freelancer arrangement review

Inputs: agreement, invoices, schedule, location, tools, substitution rights, exclusivity, manager control, client count, duration, business risk.

Steps: score employee-status indicators; identify core business work; identify control over time/place/method; check real substitution; check economic dependence; compare documents to actual practice; recommend conversion or restructuring; estimate categories of exposure; preserve neutral documentation.

## Workflow 7: Employee complaint intake

Inputs: complaint text, dates, people involved, documents, pay periods, requested remedy, retaliation concern.

Steps: acknowledge receipt without admitting liability; preserve records; classify issue; stop ongoing harm where appropriate; assign an impartial reviewer; correct undisputed payroll gaps; avoid retaliation; track timeline and outcome; escalate severe issues.

## Workflow 8: Policy migration from informal practice

Inputs: current practice, staff list, sectors, contracts, payroll exports, leave balances, pension files.

Steps: inventory practices; compare each practice to mandatory rights; prioritize wage, pension, protected-status, and termination gaps; draft neutral policy; train managers; communicate policy without waiving past claims; audit after two payroll cycles.
