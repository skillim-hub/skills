# Workflow Guide

This guide provides end-to-end workflows for planning, validating, approving, exporting, and publishing organic social content for Israeli businesses.

## Workflow 1: Two-week local service calendar

Use for salons, clinics, repairs, tutors, designers, consultants, and home-service providers.

### Inputs

```yaml
business_name: "סטודיו נועה"
business_type: "מספרה"
city: "ראשון לציון"
audience: "נשים 25-45"
platforms:
  - facebook
  - instagram
days: 14
timezone: Asia/Jerusalem
shabbat_policy: avoid
content_pillars:
  - trust
  - education
  - proof
  - local
  - offer
```

### Steps

1. Confirm service area and booking method.
2. Choose Facebook for community trust and Instagram for visual proof.
3. Create 8-10 post ideas.
4. Write captions in Hebrew.
5. Mark asset needs: photo, Reel, Story, testimonial, before/after.
6. Run validation for platform limits and consent risks.
7. Schedule only in safe Israeli windows.
8. Export the calendar for owner approval.
9. Queue approved posts.
10. Track saves, comments, DMs, and bookings.

### Example calendar

| Date | Time | Platform | Format | Pillar | Caption hook | Asset | Risk |
|---|---:|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 08/06/2026 | 08:30 | Instagram | Reel | Education | "לפני צבע? 3 דברים שכדאי לדעת" | Vertical video | Add subtitles |
| 09/06/2026 | 12:30 | Facebook | Group post | Local | "יש תורים פנויים השבוע בראשון לציון" | None | Check group rules |
| 10/06/2026 | 20:00 | Instagram | Carousel | Trust | "איך יודעים שגוון מתאים לעור?" | 5 slides | Use readable text |
| 11-06-2026 | 19:00 | Facebook | Page post | Proof | "תוצאה עדינה, לא צעקנית" | Client photo | Written consent |
| 14-06-2026 | 08:30 | Instagram | Story | Offer | "התפנה תור היום ב-16:00" | Story card | Confirm availability |

### Approval checklist

- All customer images approved.
- All prices and dates correct.
- All CTAs lead to the correct booking channel.
- Shabbat and sensitive days avoided.
- Hashtags readable.
- Owner reviewed tone and claims.

## Workflow 2: Freelancer authority plan

Use for accountants, coaches, designers, developers, writers, translators, photographers, architects, and advisors.

### Inputs

```yaml
business_type: "פרילנסרית"
profession: "רואת חשבון"
audience: "עצמאים ועסקים קטנים"
platforms:
  - linkedin
  - facebook
  - instagram
days: 30
cadence: standard
```

### Steps

1. Define 4 recurring content pillars: tax reminders, myths, practical checklists, client process.
2. Use LinkedIn for professional authority.
3. Use Facebook groups for common questions.
4. Use Instagram carousels for checklists.
5. Add a professional review note for tax advice.
6. Avoid direct promises such as "חיסכון מובטח במס".
7. Add date-specific reminders only after confirming the current deadline.
8. Export a monthly table.
9. Convert high-performing comments into next-month content.

### Example caption set

LinkedIn:

```text
עצמאים: לא כל קבלה ש"קשורה לעבודה" מוכרת אוטומטית.

לפני שמכניסים הוצאה לדוח, בדקו קשר ישיר לעסק, מסמך תקין והאם מדובר בהוצאה מעורבת.
```

Facebook group:

```text
שאלה שחוזרת הרבה אצל עצמאים בתחילת הדרך:
מה חייבים לשמור כדי לא לרדוף אחרי מסמכים בסוף שנה?

רשימה בסיסית:
חשבוניות, קבלות, אישורי ניכוי מס במקור, דוחות הפקדה ופירוט תשלומים לספקים.
```

Instagram carousel:

```text
שקופית 1: 5 מסמכים שכל עצמאי צריך לשמור
שקופית 2: חשבוניות וקבלות
שקופית 3: אישורי מס
שקופית 4: תיעוד הוצאות מעורבות
שקופית 5: שמירה חודשית במקום לחץ בדצמבר
```

### Risk controls

- Mark every tax post as general information.
- Avoid personalized advice in comments.
- Move complex questions to private consultation.
- Do not request documents through social comments.
- Store leads securely.

## Workflow 3: Retail offer with consumer-protection checks

Use for shops, ecommerce brands, pop-ups, food businesses, beauty products, workshops, and seasonal offers.

### Inputs

```yaml
business_type: "חנות"
city: "באר שבע"
offer:
  title: "פתיחת עונה"
  price: "₪199"
  valid_until: "30-06-2026"
  exclusions: "עד גמר המלאי"
platforms:
  - facebook
  - instagram
  - tiktok
```

### Steps

1. Confirm exact offer, price, validity date, quantity, shipping and cancellation terms.
2. Write one clear offer statement.
3. Adapt it for each platform.
4. Add visual asset requirements.
5. Validate consumer-protection clarity.
6. Schedule higher-value educational content before the offer post.
7. Publish Stories for reminders only after the initial post is approved.
8. Monitor comments for confusion and update FAQ.

### Offer wording template

```text
מבצע פתיחת עונה:
פריט נבחר ב-₪199 עד 30-06-2026 או עד גמר המלאי, המוקדם מביניהם.

הפרטים המלאים בקופה ובאתר.
```

### Platform adaptation

| Platform | Adaptation |
|---|---|
| Facebook | Explain terms and local pickup/shipping |
| Instagram | Use carousel: product, use case, price, terms, CTA |
| TikTok | Show product in use; say terms verbally and in captions |
| LinkedIn | Usually skip unless offer is B2B or employer-brand relevant |

### Risk controls

- Do not hide conditions in image fine print.
- Do not imply unlimited stock when stock is limited.
- Do not use fake countdowns.
- Do not use "חינם" unless truly free under applicable rules.
- Keep screenshots of the approved offer.

## Workflow 4: B2B LinkedIn campaign

Use for SaaS, agencies, consultants, HR vendors, training companies, cyber firms, and professional services.

### Inputs

```yaml
business_type: "B2B SaaS"
audience: "VP Sales and RevOps"
platforms:
  - linkedin
  - facebook
days: 21
objective: "qualified conversations"
```

### Steps

1. Define the business pain in one sentence.
2. Create 3 LinkedIn themes: insight, case lesson, framework.
3. Create 2 Facebook professional-group posts as questions or checklists.
4. Avoid direct sales in every post.
5. Prepare founder or subject-matter expert voice.
6. Validate confidentiality and claims.
7. Schedule LinkedIn on Israeli workdays.
8. Track profile views, comments, connection quality, and inbound messages.

### Example sequence

| Day | Platform | Post type | Topic |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | LinkedIn | Text insight | "Dashboard overload in sales teams" |
| 3 | Facebook group | Question | "What breaks most often between lead and follow-up?" |
| 7 | LinkedIn | Framework | "3 signals for pipeline noise" |
| 10 | LinkedIn | Case lesson | "What changed after reducing alert volume" |
| 14 | Facebook group | Checklist | "Before buying another CRM add-on" |
| 21 | LinkedIn | CTA | "Checklist available for RevOps teams" |

### LinkedIn post structure

```text
Hook: one specific claim.
Context: why it matters.
Example: short, anonymized, measurable.
Takeaway: one useful lesson.
CTA: invite a professional reply.
```

### Risk controls

- Remove client names unless approved.
- Avoid unverified percentages.
- Do not imply official partnership without permission.
- Keep confidential screenshots out of images.
- Avoid scraping commenters into outreach lists.

## Workflow 5: Emergency pause and restart

Use during national emergency, local crisis, tragedy, cyber incident, product recall, or operational disruption.

### Trigger examples

- National mourning or security event.
- Local tragedy affecting the business area.
- Public backlash in comments.
- Incorrect offer or price published.
- Customer privacy incident.
- Platform account compromise.

### Pause steps

1. Stop queued promotional posts.
2. Export the queue for review.
3. Categorize each post: safe, edit, hold, delete.
4. Publish only factual service updates if needed.
5. Avoid humor, trend content, hard sales, and automated greetings.
6. Assign one person to monitor comments.
7. Document decisions.

### Restart steps

1. Confirm the situation has stabilized.
2. Start with useful, low-pressure content.
3. Avoid aggressive offers in the first restart post.
4. Check that postponed posts are still accurate.
5. Revalidate dates, prices and availability.
6. Restart gradually.

### Example service update

```text
עדכון פעילות:
הסטודיו פתוח היום עד 16:00 בהתאם להנחיות.

לקוחות עם תור שנוח להן לדחות מוזמנות לשלוח הודעה.
שמרו על עצמכן.
```

## Workflow 6: Consent-based testimonial collection

Use when customer proof is needed without fake reviews or privacy risk.

### Steps

1. Ask for feedback after service completion.
2. Explain how the quote or image may be used.
3. Request written approval for exact wording and media.
4. Store approval with date and channel.
5. Avoid editing the quote in a way that changes meaning.
6. Offer anonymization when appropriate.
7. Add disclosure if compensation, discount, or gift was provided.

### Consent message template

```text
היי, תודה על הפרגון.
אפשר להשתמש בציטוט הזה בפייסבוק/אינסטגרם של העסק?

נשתמש בשם פרטי בלבד, בלי פרטים מזהים נוספים.
אפשר כמובן לבקש הסרה בהמשך.
```

### Before/after image checklist

- Customer consent exists.
- Image does not expose sensitive details.
- Lighting and angle are not misleading.
- Caption avoids guaranteed results.
- Professional claim reviewed if required.
- Removal request process exists.

## Workflow 7: Monthly performance review

Run at the end of each month.

### Data to collect

| Metric | Source | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| Reach | Platform insights | Topic and format awareness |
| Saves | Instagram/TikTok/LinkedIn | Educational content quality |
| Shares | Platform insights | Local relevance |
| Comments | Platform insights | Conversation topics |
| DMs | Inbox | Lead quality and FAQ gaps |
| Bookings/sales | CRM or manual log | Business impact |
| Negative feedback | Comments/DMs | Tone or claim correction |

### Review questions

- Which hooks generated saves or comments?
- Which platform created actual leads?
- Which posts caused confusion?
- Which posts required too much production time?
- Which content pillar deserves more slots?
- Which platform deserves fewer slots?
- Which compliance checks delayed approval?

### Next-cycle actions

- Keep the top two performing pillars.
- Rewrite weak hooks instead of abandoning the topic.
- Move content to the platform where it fits best.
- Reduce production-heavy formats that do not produce results.
- Add FAQs from comments to the content calendar.
- Update blackout dates and holiday planning.
