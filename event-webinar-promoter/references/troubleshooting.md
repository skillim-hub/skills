# Troubleshooting

Use this guide when a campaign is unclear, underperforming, risky, or operationally blocked.

## Fast diagnosis

| Problem | First check | Fast fix |
|---|---|---|
| Low registrations | Title and audience | Rewrite title around a practical result |
| Low attendance | Reminder cadence | Add calendar invite and 24h/2h reminders |
| High unsubscribe rate | Segment fit | Reduce frequency and narrow the audience |
| Legal/compliance concern | Consent and claims | Remove direct blasts and unverifiable claims |
| Accessibility concern | Venue/platform details | Add contact and accommodations line |
| Paid event hesitation | Terms and value | Show what is included and cancellation terms |
| Partner confusion | Ownership of message | Partner sends to its own opted-in list |
| No clear CTA | Page structure | Add one button above fold and after agenda |
| Weak Hebrew copy | Literal translation | Rewrite in natural Israeli phrasing |
| Poor ad performance | Broad targeting | Create one ad per audience segment |

## Symptom: many clicks, few registrations

Likely causes:

- The title is interesting but the page does not answer “what will be gained”.
- The form asks for too much information.
- Date, time, price, or host are hard to find.
- The CTA appears only at the bottom.
- The audience is too broad.

Fix:

1. Move date, time, price, format, and CTA above the fold.
2. Replace broad title with a concrete outcome.
3. Reduce fields to name and email unless phone is needed and consent is explicit.
4. Add “למי זה מתאים” and “מה יוצאים איתו”.
5. Add one practical credibility proof.

## Symptom: many registrations, low show-up

Likely causes:

- No calendar invite.
- Reminders are too early or too vague.
- The event has no live-only reason.
- Registration happened too far in advance without nurture.
- The time is inconvenient for the audience.

Fix:

1. Send calendar invite immediately after registration.
2. Send reminders 72h, 24h, morning-of, and 2h before.
3. Include “what to prepare” and “why attend live”.
4. Add Q&A, worksheet, or live critique.
5. Test a different hour next time.

## Symptom: WhatsApp complaints

Likely causes:

- No consent.
- Message sounds like mass advertising.
- No opt-out.
- Audience does not remember the relationship.
- Message frequency is too high.

Fix:

1. Stop bulk sending until consent basis is confirmed.
2. Add advertiser identity and opt-out route.
3. Use one-to-one messages only for real relationships.
4. Move promotion to organic posts or partner-owned channels.
5. Keep a suppression list.

## Symptom: Hebrew copy sounds unnatural

Replace:

| Avoid | Use |
|---|---|
| “וובינר עוצמתי” | “וובינר מעשי” |
| “תגלו את הסודות” | “נעבור על שיטה פשוטה” |
| “אל תפספסו הזדמנות חד פעמית” | “ההרשמה פתוחה עד [תאריך]” |
| “תמקסמו את העסק” | “תשפרו את תהליך המכירה” |
| “פניות חמים בטירוף” | “פניות רלוונטיות יותר” |

Checklist:

- Use one concrete audience.
- Use verbs like “ללמוד”, “לבנות”, “לתרגל”, “לקבל”.
- Avoid excessive exclamation marks.
- Avoid English marketing terms unless the audience uses them.
- Prefer “ללא עלות” over “חינם” in professional contexts when suitable.

## Symptom: paid event does not sell

Likely causes:

- Value is unclear.
- Price appears before outcome.
- No proof of practical output.
- Cancellation terms are missing.
- Capacity or format is unclear.
- Audience is not convinced the event fits them.

Fix:

1. Add a practical outcome: “יוצאים עם...”.
2. Show exact agenda.
3. Explain what is included.
4. Clarify VAT and receipt/invoice process where relevant.
5. Add cancellation terms.
6. Add a FAQ for common objections.
7. Show real examples or before/after materials where permitted.

## Symptom: partner campaign creates privacy risk

Likely causes:

- Partner wants to share raw list.
- Landing page privacy note does not mention organizer.
- UTM links are not separated.
- Both sides plan to email the same people.

Fix:

1. Do not exchange raw lists by default.
2. Let each party send to its own audience.
3. Use partner-specific links.
4. Add clear organizer identity.
5. Agree who receives registration data and why.
6. Document the arrangement.

## Symptom: date conflicts with Israeli routine

Common conflict types:

- Jewish holidays and holiday eves.
- School vacations.
- Friday afternoon.
- Saturday for general business audiences.
- Election days or major national events.
- Evening events during peak family routines for parent audiences.

Fix:

- Move consumer webinars to 20:00–21:30 Sunday–Thursday.
- Move B2B sessions to 10:00 or 14:00.
- Use Friday morning only for short local events.
- Add calendar invite and reminders.

## Symptom: landing page accessibility gap

Check:

- Keyboard navigation.
- Sufficient contrast.
- Clear headings.
- Text alternatives for images.
- Captions or transcript plan for recording when available.
- Venue access details.
- Contact for accommodations.
- No essential information only in an image.

Fix:

```text
להתאמות נגישות או שאלות לוגיסטיות, ניתן לפנות עד 48 שעות לפני האירוע ל-[אימייל/טלפון].
```

## Symptom: campaign has risky claims

Risky examples:

- Guaranteed income.
- Guaranteed clients.
- Guaranteed weight loss or health outcome.
- “Approved by” without proof.
- “Only method that works”.
- Fake testimonials.
- Unverifiable numbers.

Fix:

- Replace guarantee with scope.
- Use “נלמד”, “נתרגל”, “נראה דוגמאות”.
- Add factual proof only when documented.
- Remove claims about competitors.
- Keep testimonials exact and authorized.

## Symptom: recording cannot be shared

Reasons:

- Participants asked private questions.
- Chat includes personal data.
- Music/images/slides lack rights for redistribution.
- Speaker consent did not include replay.
- The event promised no recording.

Fix:

1. Share slides or summary instead.
2. Edit recording to remove sensitive parts.
3. Obtain permission before publishing.
4. Clarify replay policy for future events.

## Recovery templates

### Correction email

```text
נושא: תיקון פרטים לגבי [שם האירוע]

שלום,
בהודעה הקודמת הופיע פרט לא מדויק לגבי [הנושא].
הפרט הנכון הוא: [תיקון ברור].

שאר פרטי האירוע ללא שינוי:
[תאריך], [שעה], [קישור/מיקום].

תודה על ההבנה.
```

### Low-registration partner request

```text
היי,
יש לנו וובינר/סדנה על [נושא] ביום [תאריך], והוא יכול להתאים ל-[קהל].
אפשר לפרסם אצלך לקהל שלך עם הקישור הייעודי הזה?
[קישור UTM]

מצורף נוסח קצר שאפשר לערוך:
[נוסח]
```

### Unsubscribe handling

```text
הוסרת מרשימת העדכונים לגבי [שם העסק/אירוע].
לא יישלחו אליך עדכונים נוספים בערוץ הזה, למעט הודעות תפעוליות אם קיימת הרשמה פעילה.
```

## Escalation checklist

Escalate to a qualified professional when:

- The campaign includes sensitive personal data.
- The event is medical, financial, legal, employment-related, or directed to minors.
- Paid consumer terms are complex.
- Partners share registration data.
- The event is recorded and redistributed.
- Accessibility obligations are unclear.
- A complaint or legal notice arrives.
