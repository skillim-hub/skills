# Troubleshooting Guide

## First triage questions

1. How many days remain?
2. Is the prescription visible as active?
3. Are repeats left?
4. Is the validity date still current?
5. Is the issue renewal, stock, payment, pickup, delivery, or portal access?
6. Is another person involved?

## Renewal button missing

Likely causes:
- No repeats.
- Expired prescription.
- Medication appears only in history.
- Wrong profile.
- Doctor review or lab test required.

Actions:
1. Verify profile.
2. Check active prescriptions, not history only.
3. Send doctor or clinic request.
4. Call if supply is under 3 days.

## Doctor did not respond

Actions:
1. Verify submission confirmation.
2. Check clinic hours and holidays.
3. Call the clinic if supply is low.
4. Ask whether another clinic role can route the request.
5. Record date and support reference.

## Pharmacy cannot see prescription

Actions:
1. Recheck active status.
2. Confirm correct patient profile.
3. Wait briefly for synchronization.
4. Call HMO support or clinic if unresolved.
5. Avoid sending screenshots unless requested through a secure channel.

## Delivery unavailable

Actions:
1. Try pickup.
2. Try another licensed pharmacy.
3. Ask whether stock can be reserved.
4. Prefer pickup when supply is under 3 days.

## Price higher than expected

Actions:
1. Ask for itemized price in ₪.
2. Confirm HMO discount.
3. Ask whether generic substitution is available and allowed.
4. Do not pay until price and fulfillment terms are clear.

## Stock unavailable

Actions:
1. Call nearby branches.
2. Call local pharmacies.
3. Ask when stock is expected.
4. Ask the clinician about appropriate alternatives.
5. Escalate when supply is critical.

## Portal login problem

Actions:
1. Let the user log in directly.
2. Do not collect passwords or one-time codes.
3. Use official reset or support.
4. Continue only with safe information the user provides after login.

## Caregiver authorization problem

Actions:
1. Stop if consent is missing.
2. Use official caregiver or dependent access.
3. Ask pharmacy what proof is needed.
4. Do not store ID images.

## Cold-chain delivery failed

Actions:
1. Do not assume the medicine is usable.
2. Contact the pharmacy immediately.
3. Ask whether replacement, pickup, or safe-use confirmation is required.
4. Record time and instructions.

## Red flags

Escalate to clinical or emergency help when:
- Severe symptoms appear.
- No supply remains for a critical ongoing treatment.
- The user is confused about medication identity or dose.
- A child, pregnant patient, elderly patient, or complex chronic patient faces interruption.
- Pharmacy cannot dispense and no supply remains.

## Safe logs

Keep:
- Case date
- Channel
- Confirmation number
- Follow-up date
- Pharmacy branch
- Price in ₪
- Delivery or pickup status

Avoid:
- Passwords
- One-time codes
- Full ID number
- Diagnosis
- Full prescription files
- Payment card details


## Be or Newpharm support cannot be confirmed

Actions:
1. Do not assume prescription delivery is available.
2. Check the current app, branch, or pharmacist.
3. Ask whether the service supports prescription medication for the user’s Kupat Cholim.
4. Ask whether pickup is required.
5. Switch to a verified HMO pharmacy, Super-Pharm route, or local licensed pharmacy when uncertain.
