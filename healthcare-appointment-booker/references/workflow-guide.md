# Workflow Guide

## 1. Routine family doctor

1. Confirm HMO, city, date range, and urgency.
2. Screen for emergency red flags.
3. Search official app/site for `רופא משפחה`.
4. Compare first available, preferred clinic, language, and accessibility.
5. If no slot appears, expand area or call clinic desk.
6. Save confirmation and cancellation deadline.

Hebrew script:

```text
שלום, אני מבקש/ת לקבוע תור לרופא/ת משפחה באזור <עיר>,
בין <DD/MM/YYYY> ל-<DD/MM/YYYY>. מה התור הראשון הזמין?
```

## 2. Same-day pediatric care

1. Screen for severe symptoms and infant fever.
2. If emergency red flags exist, route to emergency/urgent care.
3. Search pediatrician today.
4. Check urgent pediatric clinic, telehealth, or nurse triage.
5. Confirm guardian attendance and documents.

## 3. Specialist with referral

1. Read only specialty, validity, restrictions, and approval needs.
2. Search official app/site by specialty and city.
3. Apply language and accessibility preferences.
4. If no slots, expand city and call service center.
5. Confirm documents and cancellation rules.

## 4. Specialist without referral

1. Search whether direct booking is allowed.
2. If blocked, book family doctor or submit digital referral request.
3. Prepare a truthful one-sentence reason.
4. Retry specialist booking after referral approval.

## 5. Imaging

1. Identify MRI, CT, ultrasound, X-ray, mammography, or other imaging.
2. Confirm valid order/referral.
3. Check approval/Form 17 and contracted institute.
4. Confirm preparation: fasting, contrast, metal implants, pregnancy questions, arrival time.
5. Book official slot and save confirmation.

## 6. Lab tests

1. Confirm active order.
2. Check whether appointment is needed or walk-in allowed.
3. Confirm fasting and medication instructions through the official channel.
4. Select clinic and time.
5. Do not interpret results.

## 7. Nurse appointment

1. Identify vaccine, injection, wound care, pregnancy nurse, diabetes nurse, or blood pressure.
2. Check whether doctor order/materials are required.
3. Search nurse slot or call clinic desk.
4. Confirm preparation and accessibility.

## 8. Administrative approval

1. Identify Form 17, commitment, external provider, or district approval.
2. Send user to official HMO administrative approvals channel.
3. Upload documents only through official portal.
4. Track submission date.
5. Continue booking after approval.

## 9. No availability

1. Expand city radius.
2. Remove provider preference.
3. Check all time windows.
4. Try telehealth when suitable.
5. Call clinic desk for cancellations.
6. Call HMO center for district options.
7. Use urgent clinic or nurse triage for time-sensitive clinical needs.
8. Never use scraping or queue-bypass automation.

## 10. Cancellation/reschedule

1. Verify current appointment and cancellation deadline.
2. Reschedule before canceling if medically important.
3. Cancel duplicate appointment through official channel.
4. Save cancellation confirmation.
5. Notify caregiver/employer only with permission.

## 11. Caregiver booking

1. Confirm the patient authorized help.
2. Have the patient enter credentials directly.
3. Use a neutral script.
4. Store only appointment metadata.
5. Delete notes after the retention period.

## 12. Paid small-business assistance

1. State scope as administrative booking help only.
2. Disclose price in ₪ before work starts.
3. Do not guarantee slot availability.
4. Do not provide medical advice.
5. Issue receipt/invoice where required.
6. Use official channels only.
7. Delete or redact sensitive notes.

Example:

```text
סיוע אדמיניסטרטיבי בקביעת תור בקופת חולים: ₪45 כולל מע״מ, אם חל.
שיעור המע״מ המאומת ל-04/06/2026 הוא 18%.
השירות אינו כולל ייעוץ רפואי, אבחון או התחייבות לזמינות תורים.
```
