# Troubleshooting guide

## Symptom: Arnona bill increased sharply

Likely causes:

- Annual rate update.
- Zone changed.
- Property classification changed.
- Measured area increased.
- Discount expired.
- Vacancy exemption ended.
- Prior credit was consumed.
- Arrears, interest, linkage, or collection fees were added.

Action:

1. Compare current bill to the prior bill line by line.
2. Identify which field changed.
3. Ask the municipality for the decision or inspection report behind the change.
4. Verify the change against the annual Arnona order.
5. File an objection if the changed field is wrong and the deadline is open.

## Symptom: Business is charged for the full apartment

Likely causes:

- Business registration at home triggered inspection.
- Municipality found signs, clients, stock, or dedicated work area.
- The local order treats certain home activities as business use.

Action:

1. Mark the actual business area on a floor plan.
2. Gather photos showing residential use of the rest of the apartment.
3. Review mixed-use rules in the local order.
4. Request split classification if supported.
5. Avoid false statements about business activity.

## Symptom: Taxable area exceeds Tabu area

Likely causes:

- Arnona uses gross measurement.
- Shared areas are included.
- Balconies, storage, parking, galleries, or external walls are included.
- Tabu plan is outdated or net-only.

Action:

1. Read the measurement clause in the municipal order.
2. Request municipal measurement details.
3. Compare item by item.
4. Use a licensed measurer or appraiser for material differences.
5. Challenge only the components not permitted by the order.

## Symptom: Discount application rejected

Likely causes:

- Missing document.
- Wrong holder.
- Not primary residence.
- Income exceeds threshold.
- Area cap reached.
- Application submitted after deadline.
- Discount category is discretionary and committee rejected it.

Action:

1. Ask for rejection reason in writing.
2. Match each missing requirement to a document.
3. Submit corrected application promptly.
4. Check whether retroactive application is allowed.
5. Consider appeal or municipal discount committee route if available.

## Symptom: Purchase-tax estimate differs from lawyer calculation

Likely causes:

- Different bracket year.
- Buyer treated as additional-home owner.
- Family-unit rules applied.
- Replacement-home sale period considered.
- Relief category accepted or rejected.
- Price includes linked obligations or fixtures.

Action:

1. Align the contract date.
2. Use the same Tax Authority bracket source.
3. Compare buyer-profile assumptions.
4. Ask for a marginal bracket table.
5. Treat the filing calculation as controlling unless corrected by updated official guidance.

## Symptom: Betterment levy arrives unexpectedly before sale

Likely causes:

- Rights were improved by an old plan.
- Permit request triggered realization.
- Use change or relief triggered charge.
- Municipality issued pre-sale certificate condition.
- Prior owner did not settle all levy.

Action:

1. Identify plan number and approval date.
2. Identify realization event.
3. Ask for committee appraiser report.
4. Verify ownership share and exemption eligibility.
5. Consider counter-appraisal within the procedural deadline.

## Symptom: Mas Rechush claim is delayed

Likely causes:

- Missing claim number.
- Missing photos or invoices.
- Damage not connected clearly to recognized event.
- Appraiser inspection pending.
- Bank/account or ownership proof incomplete.
- Claim submitted under wrong track.

Action:

1. Collect claim confirmation.
2. Prepare a chronological evidence folder.
3. Submit missing documents through the official route.
4. Keep damaged items or replacement records.
5. Track all communications.

## Symptom: Municipality refuses to change holder

Likely causes:

- Missing lease or sale document.
- No handover date.
- Prior tenant debt.
- Property still occupied by old holder.
- Municipal form not signed by required parties.

Action:

1. Provide signed lease/sale agreement and possession protocol.
2. Provide ID/company number and contact details of incoming holder.
3. Separate old debt from new current charges.
4. Request account ledger and written refusal reason.
5. Consider objection if charged for a period without possession.

## Symptom: Empty property still billed

Likely causes:

- Vacancy exemption not requested.
- Property contains furniture or equipment.
- Exemption already used.
- Local limit expired.
- Property is considered usable despite no tenant.

Action:

1. Check local vacant-property exemption rule.
2. File form immediately.
3. Attach photos and utility readings.
4. Request inspection.
5. Calendar exemption end date.

## Symptom: CLI calculation is not accepted by authority

Cause:

The helper script is an advisory calculator with sample data. It is not an official assessment.

Action:

1. Use the CLI to structure facts and sanity-check arithmetic.
2. Replace sample rates with official rates before filing.
3. Cite the current official source.
4. Attach authority documents, not CLI output alone.

## Escalation triggers

Refer for professional review when any condition applies:

- Property transaction above a material amount.
- Betterment levy assessment received.
- Administrative petition may be required.
- Family-unit ownership is complex.
- Trust, company, inheritance, gift, combination transaction, or foreign resident issue exists.
- Significant retroactive Arnona demand.
- Enforcement or lien has started.
- Damage claim is high-value or disputed.
