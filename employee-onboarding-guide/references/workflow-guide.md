# Workflow Guide

## Workflow 1: Standard salaried employee

Inputs: full name, start date, role, manager, gross monthly salary in ₪, work location, schedule, other-employer status, and pension status.

Steps:

1. Create employee record.
2. Send document request, including Form 101 submission within 7 days of start.
3. Draft written terms and deliver them within 30 days for adults or 7 days for employees under 18.
4. Collect Form 101, ID or passport, bank details, and pension details.
5. Check whether coordination approvals are required.
6. Send payroll handoff.
7. Prepare access and equipment.
8. Deliver compliance and role training.
9. Hold 7-day and 30-day check-ins.

Acceptance criteria:

- Form 101 is received or payroll has handling instructions.
- Bank details are received.
- Written terms are drafted or delivered.
- Pension status is documented.
- Payroll has all facts before payroll close.

## Workflow 2: First employee in a small business

1. Confirm payroll provider or payroll software.
2. Confirm employer reporting setup with the accountant or payroll provider.
3. Create restricted employee-record storage.
4. Select timekeeping and leave-tracking method.
5. Prepare written terms template.
6. Send employee document request.
7. Prepare minimum policies: attendance, expenses, confidentiality, privacy, safety, and anti-harassment.
8. Schedule payroll-close reminders.

Risk controls:

- Do not run payroll manually without professional setup.
- Do not promise net salary without payroll calculation.
- Do not store employee documents in a shared open folder.

## Workflow 3: Employee with another employer

1. Confirm the employee declared additional income on Form 101.
2. Request tax coordination approval.
3. Ask payroll whether National Insurance coordination is required.
4. Record missing approvals as payroll risk.
5. Ask payroll how to process until approvals arrive.
6. Add annual renewal reminder.

## Workflow 4: Active pension arrangement

1. Ask whether an active arrangement exists.
2. Collect fund name, account or policy number, provider contact, and continuity evidence if needed.
3. Send details to payroll or a licensed pension professional.
4. Confirm first deposit deadline.
5. Add reminder before the deadline.
6. Store confirmation in restricted storage.

## Workflow 5: Hourly or shift employee

1. Draft written terms with hourly rate in ₪ and work pattern, then set attendance records before the first shift.
2. Train employee on clock-in, breaks, absence, and corrections.
3. Set manager approval route.
4. Add overtime and holiday-work review warning.
5. Send approved hours to payroll before close.

## Workflow 6: Remote employee

1. Prepare remote-work terms.
2. List equipment and serial numbers.
3. Approve access using least privilege.
4. Train on privacy, confidentiality, phishing, and incident reporting.
5. Define work hours and availability.
6. Schedule equipment return and access removal for offboarding.

## Workflow 7: Freelancer converting to employee

1. Separate past invoices from future employment.
2. Confirm final supplier invoice period.
3. Draft employee terms with gross salary.
4. Collect employee payroll documents.
5. Update access from supplier profile to employee profile.
6. Review intellectual property and confidentiality continuity.
7. Revoke unneeded supplier permissions.

## Workflow 8: Youth employee

1. Verify age.
2. Check permitted hours and prohibited tasks.
3. Provide safety training before first shift.
4. Assign adult supervision.
5. Monitor schedule and rest days.
6. Keep training evidence.

## Workflow 9: Foreign worker

1. Pause standard onboarding.
2. Verify work permit, visa, employer authorization, medical insurance, written contract, suitable housing where relevant, pension or deposit route, and sector rules.
3. Confirm health insurance.
4. Confirm payroll, tax, National Insurance, and housing rules where relevant.
5. Provide instructions in a language the employee understands.
6. Store permit evidence securely.

## Workflow 10: Complete onboarding packet

Packet contents:

- Cover page.
- Employee document request.
- Written terms checklist.
- Payroll handoff.
- Pension intake.
- Equipment and access form.
- Policies and acknowledgments.
- First-day agenda.
- First-month training plan.
- 7-day and 30-day check-ins.
- Professional-review list.
