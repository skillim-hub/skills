# Workflow Guide

Use these workflows as operational playbooks. Treat amounts, thresholds, and filing deadlines as verification items.

## Workflow 1: Freelancer onboarding

1. Classify activity: business, profession, occasional transaction, salary, royalty, rent, or capital gain.
2. Estimate annual turnover using contracts, expected hours, platform projections, and recurring clients.
3. Check whether exempt dealer status is available for the profession.
4. Register for VAT as exempt dealer or authorized dealer according to current law.
5. Open or update income-tax file.
6. Check National Insurance classification and expected advances.
7. Choose bookkeeping method with a professional adviser where required.
8. Create document templates: receipt, tax invoice, invoice-receipt, credit invoice.
9. Open a dedicated bank account or tag business transactions clearly.
10. Set monthly review: income, expenses, VAT, withholding certificates, advances.

Professional-help triggers: regulated profession, foreign clients, foreign platforms, employees, expected turnover near threshold, prior unreported income, or family participation.

## Workflow 2: VAT status and document selection

1. Confirm VAT status.
2. Confirm transaction type and date.
3. Confirm whether payment was received.
4. Confirm whether VAT is charged.
5. Choose document type: exempt dealer receipt, authorized dealer tax invoice or invoice-receipt, matched credit invoice for corrections.
6. Validate business name, tax number, customer, date, description, amount, VAT, and serial number.

Common mistakes: exempt dealer issues tax invoice, authorized dealer issues receipt without required tax invoice, credit note lacks original reference, invoice names wrong entity.

## Workflow 3: Foreign-client VAT review

1. Identify contracting customer and actual beneficiary.
2. Confirm customer residency and business registration abroad.
3. Check whether an Israeli resident receives or benefits from the service.
4. Determine where the service is used.
5. Review contract, statement of work, deliverables, correspondence, and payment.
6. Decide whether zero-rate analysis is plausible or full VAT is safer.
7. Prepare invoice wording and documentation pack.
8. Recommend professional review for material amounts or mixed Israeli benefit.

## Workflow 4: Expense deductibility review

1. Identify expense type and amount.
2. Link expense to income-producing activity.
3. Check if the expense is private, capital, mixed, or current.
4. Validate invoice or receipt.
5. Determine allocation method for mixed use.
6. Check VAT input restrictions separately from income-tax deduction.
7. Record treatment in an expense memo.

## Workflow 5: Withholding-tax certificate check

1. Identify payer and payee.
2. Ask whether the payee has current approvals for bookkeeping and withholding.
3. Check certificate validity dates.
4. Determine payment type.
5. Compare deducted amount with certificate terms.
6. Explain that withheld tax is usually a credit against final liability, not an additional tax.
7. Preserve certificate and payment statement.

## Workflow 6: Annual return preparation

1. Identify salary, business, rental, capital gains, foreign income, pension, grants, and spouse income.
2. Identify tax file status.
3. Collect Forms 106, 867, withholding certificates, receipts, expense documents, rental agreements, and foreign statements.
4. Check whether filing exemption applies or a return is mandatory.
5. Summarize deductions, credits, losses, and payments on account.
6. Mark issues requiring accountant review.

## Workflow 7: Residential rental income

1. Confirm asset type: residential or commercial.
2. Confirm monthly rent and number of properties.
3. Check personal or company ownership.
4. Compare exemption track, reduced-rate track, and ordinary income track where applicable.
5. Consider expense deduction and depreciation consequences.
6. Flag VAT if commercial or business-like letting may apply.
7. Check National Insurance exposure.

## Workflow 8: Real-estate sale

1. Identify asset: apartment, land, commercial unit, rights, or shares in real-estate company.
2. Collect purchase agreement, acquisition tax assessment, expenses, mortgage fees, legal fees, renovation invoices, and valuation.
3. Check residence, family-unit holdings, previous exemptions, inherited property, gift history, and construction rights.
4. Calculate indicative gain only if data is complete.
5. Check possible exemption, linear relief, or ordinary betterment tax.
6. Prepare reporting calendar and payment expectations.
7. Check municipal betterment levy separately.
8. Escalate before signing if exemption is material.

## Workflow 9: Purchase-tax estimate

1. Identify purchaser: Israeli resident, foreign resident, company, trustee, disabled person, new immigrant, or family unit.
2. Identify property type.
3. Determine whether another apartment is owned.
4. Verify current brackets and reliefs.
5. Use the price and bracket table to produce an illustrative estimate only when current brackets are verified.
6. Flag deadlines after signing.

## Workflow 10: Tax Authority letter response

1. Identify issuing unit: income tax, VAT, land taxation, or collections.
2. Extract date, deadline, file number, period, demanded amount, and requested documents.
3. Classify urgency.
4. Prepare document index.
5. Draft a factual response outline without admissions beyond verified facts.
6. Recommend representative review for assessments, audits, suspected false books, or large amounts.


## Web-validated 2026 checkpoints

- For VAT registration, check the 2026 exempt-dealer ceiling of ₪122,833 before choosing exempt-dealer treatment.
- For VAT calculations, use the standard VAT rate of 18% from 01/01/2025 unless a specific exemption or zero-rate rule applies.
- For 2025 individual annual returns filed in 2026, verify Form 1301 and the filing dates published by the Tax Authority before advising on deadlines.
- For residential rental income, check the ₪5,654 monthly exemption ceiling and the section 122 10% track before comparing tax routes.
- For purchase-tax examples, use the official simulator instead of hand-calculating final liability; the bracket snapshot is documented in `api-reference.md`.
- For real-estate transactions, include the 30-day declaration requirement in every seller or buyer workflow.
- For Israel Invoice allocation-number questions, explain that official API documentation is registration-gated and that this package does not call government APIs.
