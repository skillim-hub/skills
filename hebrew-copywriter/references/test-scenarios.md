# Test scenarios

Use these scenarios to validate the skill, helper client, CLI, and copy quality.

1. **Local plumber landing page** — Emergency plumbing repairs in Tel Aviv, homeowners, `החל מ-₪280`. Expect warm direct Hebrew, service area, no `הכי זול`.
2. **Accountant for freelancers** — Monthly bookkeeping for `עוסק פטור` and `עוסק מורשה`, `₪350 לא כולל מע"מ`. Expect correct tax/accounting terms and no guaranteed tax savings.
3. **WhatsApp salon promotion** — Existing clients receive color appointment promo before a holiday. Expect short text, unsubscribe, date in DD/MM/YYYY if supplied.
4. **Fitness trainer after birth** — Pilates for women after birth. Expect feminine plural, sensitivity, no body-shaming, no medical guarantee.
5. **Ecommerce backpack page** — School backpack, 28 liters, padded straps, `₪179 כולל מע"מ`. Expect parent-oriented benefits and material facts.
6. **תוכנה כשירות Google Ads** — CRM for small businesses. Expect compact headlines, clear benefit, no `מספר 1`.
7. **Lawyer landing page** — Employment lawyer consultation. Expect cautious professional tone and no guaranteed legal result.
8. **Therapist lead form** — Clinic landing page with contact form. Expect privacy microcopy and no cure promise.
9. **Real estate ad** — New apartments in Netanya, limited inventory. Expect date-qualified availability and no unverified scarcity.
10. **Restaurant SMS** — Pizza shop Friday discount. Expect concise SMS, condition, unsubscribe.
11. **Nonprofit donation page** — Food baskets for families. Expect dignity-first language and clear donation amounts.
12. **Tech freelancer LinkedIn post** — Automation consultant for ops teams. Expect professional Hebrew with accepted tech terms and concrete outcomes.
13. **Mixed city municipal notice** — Hebrew announcement for multilingual residents. Expect simple Hebrew, dates, location, respectful institutional tone.
14. **Premium jewelry Instagram** — Handmade gold jewelry. Expect quiet premium register, material detail, minimal emoji.
15. **Child education course** — Gifted exam prep. Expect no guaranteed acceptance, parent-friendly clarity.
16. **Subscription renewal email** — Learning subscription, `₪79 לחודש`. Expect cancellation reference and no hidden renewal.
17. **Photographer quote follow-up** — Wedding proposal. Expect warm professional tone, package details, date dependency.
18. **Translated copy rewrite** — `גלה את הפתרון האולטימטיבי שמעצים את המסע העסקי שלך`. Expect natural Hebrew and concrete benefit.
19. **Accessibility form error** — Invalid phone number. Expect specific, non-blaming remediation.
20. **Finance lead magnet** — Mortgage checklist form. Expect privacy note and no approval promise.
21. **Holiday gift box campaign** — Passover gift boxes. Expect `בתוקף עד DD/MM/YYYY או עד גמר המלאי`.
22. **עסקים operations case study** — Factory process improvement. Expect real metrics request and no fabricated numbers.
23. **Clinic cancellation reminder** — Appointment policy. Expect clear 24-hour time and respectful tone.
24. **Course ad installments** — Digital marketing course, `₪2,400`, 6 installments. Expect no income guarantee.
25. **Review request** — Marketplace seller asks for reviews. Expect honest request, no incentive unless terms are clear.

## Acceptance checks

- Hebrew is idiomatic.
- No fabricated facts.
- No unsupported legal, medical, finance, tax, ranking, or performance claims.
- Prices use ₪.
- Dates use DD/MM/YYYY.
- הנעה לפעולה is clear.
- Gender form is deliberate.
- Compliance notes appear when needed.
