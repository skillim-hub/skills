# Test Scenarios

Use these scenarios for tabletop drills, staff onboarding, and package tests.

## 1. Unresponsive adult at reception

Expected: Call 101, bring AED, check breathing, start CPR if not breathing normally.

## 2. Unconscious but breathing worker

Expected: Call 101, recovery position if safe, monitor breathing.

## 3. Chest pain during exercise

Expected: Call 101, rest, prepare medication and allergy details.

## 4. Suspected stroke

Expected: Call 101, record last-known-well time.

## 5. Severe bleeding from glass

Expected: Call 101, direct pressure, pressure bandage.

## 6. Kitchen burn

Expected: Cool water, remove rings, cover, call 101 for serious burn.

## 7. Chemical splash

Expected: Rinse with water, keep label, call 101 for serious exposure.

## 8. Food allergy

Expected: Call 101 for red flags, help use prescribed auto-injector when indicated.

## 9. Choking adult

Expected: Call 101, trained abdominal thrusts, CPR if collapse.

## 10. Choking infant

Expected: Call 101, 5 back blows and 5 chest thrusts.

## 11. Seizure over five minutes

Expected: Call 101, protect from injury, do not restrain.

## 12. Electric shock

Expected: Cut power first, call 103 for hazard and 101 for injury.

## 13. Heat illness

Expected: Move to shade, cool, call 101 for confusion or collapse.

## 14. Fall from ladder

Expected: Call 101 for serious injury, do not move unless danger exists.

## 15. Locked gate

Expected: Access notes guide responders, staff meets ambulance.

## 16. Contact unreachable

Expected: Message primary contact, call secondary, document attempts.

## 17. Ambulance refusal

Expected: Call 101 for red flags and document dispatcher guidance.

## 18. Minor cut

Expected: Clean, dress, monitor, document workplace incident when required.

## 19. Language barrier

Expected: Use simple language and share only operational details.

## 20. Multiple casualties

Expected: Call 101, report number injured and hazards, prioritize life threats.

## 21. Missing review date

Expected: Validation fails and profile is not printed.

## 22. ID-like value in notes

Expected: Strict privacy validation fails.

## 23. AED moved

Expected: Monthly check catches change and sheets are updated.

## 24. Former employee listed

Expected: Quarterly review removes stale contact.

## 25. Unverified volunteer number

Expected: Call 101 first and remove unverified number.

## 26. Child head injury

Expected: Call 101 for vomiting or reduced consciousness, then guardian.

## 27. Medication uncertainty

Expected: Do not give medication unless prescribed or instructed.

## 28. Public export requested

Expected: Redacted export omits medical notes and consent details.

## 29. Sandbox profile id chain

Expected: Create command returns id, validate command uses same id.

## 30. Production environment flag

Expected: Examples accept `--env production` and still keep data local.
