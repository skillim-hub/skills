# End-to-End Workflow Guide

## Workflow 1: Consumer selling a single apartment

### Goal

Estimate whether tax may be due and prepare a professional handoff pack.

### Steps

1. Confirm seller identity and residency.
2. Ask whether the seller owns another apartment or a share in another apartment in Israel or abroad.
3. Confirm the apartment is a qualifying residential apartment.
4. Collect:
   - Purchase contract.
   - Sale draft or signed sale contract.
   - Purchase tax assessment.
   - Legal and broker invoices.
   - Renovation invoices.
   - Mortgage/registration expenses where relevant.
5. Run the calculator with residential settings.
6. Check single-apartment exemption before applying linear relief.
7. If exemption appears possible, present the estimate as “tax may be zero if conditions are met”.
8. If exemption is uncertain, show both:
   - No exemption / linear relief estimate.
   - Full exemption scenario.
9. Produce a document checklist and questions for the lawyer.

### Output

- Estimated tax.
- Missing facts.
- Exemption risks.
- Document pack.

## Workflow 2: Freelancer selling a home office apartment

### Goal

Separate residential and business-use issues.

### Steps

1. Identify whether any part of the apartment was used for a business, clinic, studio, or office.
2. Ask whether expenses or depreciation were claimed.
3. Collect annual depreciation schedules or tax returns if available.
4. Estimate full-property gain.
5. Add back depreciation where required.
6. Flag that residential exemption may be affected.
7. Prepare a professional review note.

### Output

- Residential estimate.
- Business-use warning.
- Depreciation list.
- Questions for accountant.

## Workflow 3: Small business selling a shop or office

### Goal

Estimate capital gain and identify depreciation/VAT/company issues.

### Steps

1. Confirm seller type: individual, licensed dealer, company, partnership.
2. Collect purchase and sale contracts.
3. Collect fixed-asset ledger.
4. Collect depreciation claimed.
5. Collect improvement invoices.
6. Enter property type as `commercial`.
7. Do not apply residential exemptions or linear apartment relief.
8. Run the gain estimate.
9. Flag VAT and business-income classification issues.
10. Prepare accountant handoff.

### Output

- Gain and estimated tax under assumed rate.
- Depreciation warning.
- VAT review warning.
- Asset ledger checklist.

## Workflow 4: Inherited apartment

### Goal

Check possible inherited-apartment exemption and continuity facts.

### Steps

1. Collect probate order or inheritance order.
2. Identify the deceased’s ownership at death.
3. Ask whether the deceased had one residential apartment.
4. Ask whether the deceased would have qualified for an exemption.
5. Identify heirs and their ownership shares.
6. Check whether money changed hands between heirs.
7. Run a seller-level calculation for each heir.
8. Show exemption scenario only if all key facts are present.
9. Otherwise run linear/gain estimate using historic basis.

### Output

- Per-heir estimate.
- Missing inheritance facts.
- Documents required for exemption claim.

## Workflow 5: Gifted apartment

### Goal

Avoid accidental fresh-basis treatment.

### Steps

1. Identify donor and recipient relationship.
2. Collect gift agreement and transfer reports.
3. Collect donor purchase date and cost.
4. Check cooling-off period facts.
5. Use donor historical basis unless professional review says otherwise.
6. Run the estimate.
7. Mark exemption as unavailable until conditions are confirmed.

### Output

- Continuity-basis calculation.
- Cooling-off questions.
- Donor-document checklist.

## Workflow 6: Land sold by a family

### Goal

Calculate each owner separately.

### Steps

1. Identify all owners and exact shares.
2. Collect historical purchase or inheritance data.
3. Confirm whether land includes building rights.
4. Collect betterment levy assessment.
5. Run property-level calculation.
6. Allocate by ownership share.
7. For different acquisition dates, run separate calculations by owner.
8. Prepare land-specific warnings.

### Output

- Per-owner gain.
- Betterment levy treatment.
- Land registry checklist.

## Workflow 7: Urban renewal transaction

### Goal

Collect facts before calculation.

### Steps

1. Classify the project: Tama 38, demolition/rebuild, evacuation-construction, combination transaction.
2. Collect developer agreement.
3. Identify cash consideration and non-cash construction services.
4. Identify temporary rent payments.
5. Identify new apartment rights.
6. Identify betterment levy and municipal approvals.
7. Do not produce a single simplified tax estimate unless the transaction has been professionally classified.
8. Provide a document checklist and risk memo.

### Output

- “Advanced review required” note.
- Data request list.
- Preliminary taxable components list.

## Workflow 8: Pre-sale planning

### Goal

Help decide whether more information is needed before signing.

### Steps

1. Use estimated sale price and planned sale date.
2. Run a conservative estimate without exemption.
3. Run an exemption scenario only if facts are strong.
4. Identify missing invoices that may reduce tax.
5. Identify whether sale timing affects linear share or exemption eligibility.
6. Provide questions for the contract lawyer.

### Output

- Conservative tax estimate.
- Potential savings from documents/exemption.
- Pre-signing action list.

## Workflow 9: Post-assessment review

### Goal

Compare user estimate to tax authority assessment.

### Steps

1. Ask for the assessment summary.
2. Compare sale value, purchase value, deductions, depreciation, CPI, and exemption status.
3. Identify differences.
4. Prepare questions for representative.
5. Do not recommend objection deadlines without checking current official dates.

### Output

- Reconciliation table.
- Difference explanations.
- Professional escalation list.
